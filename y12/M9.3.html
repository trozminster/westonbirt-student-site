Constant Acceleration Formulae 1

With constant acceleration, one straight-line velocity-time graph gives two formulae that replace drawing the graph each time. The next lesson adds three more, so that any constant acceleration problem can be solved by choosing the right one.

By the end you’ll know…
  • The five quantities are s (displacement), u (initial velocity), v (final velocity), a (acceleration) and t (time).
  • With constant acceleration, s =12(u+v)t and v=u+at; both come from the velocity-time graph.
  • Choose a positive direction, convert to SI units, and list the values you know before choosing a formula.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Five quantities, one graph

Constant acceleration problems link five quantities: the displacement s, the initial velocity u, the final velocity v, the acceleration a and the time t. A straight velocity-time graph from u to v over time t shows all five.

The gradient gives v=u+at

The gradient of the line is the acceleration, so a=v−ut. Rearranging gives v=u+at.

The area gives s =12(u+v)t

The area under the line is a trapezium with parallel sides u and v, a distance t apart. So the displacement is s =12(u+v)t.

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

List, then choose

Write down the values of s, u, v, a and t that you know, and the one you want. Choose the formula that holds exactly the letters on your list.

SI units first

Every value must be in metres and seconds before it goes into a formula. A speed in kmh−1 becomes m s−1 by dividing by 3.6

Choose a positive direction

Take the direction of motion as positive. A body that is slowing down then has a negative acceleration.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
A car moving at 8ms−1 accelerates at 1.5ms−2 for 6 s. Find its final velocity.
s is not given and not asked for, so use v=u+at.
List the valuesu=8
a=1.5
t=6
Substitutev=8+1.5×6
Final velocityv=17ms−1
WORKED EXAMPLE D
A train slows steadily from 90kmh−1 to rest over 500 m. Find the time it takes.
a is not given and not asked for, so use s =12(u+v)t, after converting.
Convert to m s−1u=25
v=0
s=500
Substitute500=12(25+0)t
Timet=40 s
