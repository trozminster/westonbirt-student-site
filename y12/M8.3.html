Quantities and Units

Every mechanics answer is a number with a unit, and the formulae only work when every quantity is in SI units. Converting first, and keeping mass and weight apart, is what the kinematics and forces chapters build on next.

By the end you’ll know…
  • The SI base units are the kilogram (kg) for mass, the metre (m) for length and the second (s) for time.
  • Derived units are built from them: velocity in ms−1, acceleration in ms−2 and force in newtons (N), where 1N=1 kg ms−2.
  • Weight is the force of gravity on a mass: W=mg, where g=9.8ms−2.
By the end you can
Convert quantities into SI units, such as km h−1 to m s−1 and grams to kilograms.
Use W=mg to find the weight of a body, or its mass from its weight.

Three base units

Mechanics measures mass in kilograms, length in metres and time in seconds. Every other unit is built from these three.

Convert one unit at a time

A compound unit is converted one part at a time. Change kilometres to metres, then hours to seconds.

Check the size of the answer

A metre per second is a larger unit of speed than a kilometre per hour. So the same speed is a smaller number in ms−1.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
Convert 54kmh−1 into m s−1.
Kilometres to metres is × 1000
hours to seconds is ÷ 3600
Kilometres to metres54×1000=54000mh−1
Hours to seconds54000÷3600=15ms−1
WORKED EXAMPLE B
Convert 25920kmh−2 into m s−2.
The hours are squared, so divide by 3600 twice.
Kilometres to metres25920×1000=25920000mh−2
Hours to seconds, twice25920000÷36002=2ms−2

Mass and weight are different

Mass is the amount of matter in a body, measured in kilograms. Weight is the force of gravity on it, measured in newtons.

Weight is mass times g

The weight of a mass of m kg is W=mg newtons, where g=9.8ms−2. Divide a weight by g to get back to the mass.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
Find the weight of a 3.5 kg bag.
Weight is a force, so the answer is in newtons.
Multiply by gW=3.5×9.8
Weight34.3 N
WORKED EXAMPLE D
A parcel has weight 14.7 N. Find its mass in grams.
Divide by g for kilograms, then change to grams.
Divide by gm=14.7÷9.8
=1.5 kg
