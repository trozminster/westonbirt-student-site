Force Vectors and Newton's Second Law

Forces are vectors, so several forces combine into one resultant, and Newton's second law says that resultant decides how a body accelerates. From here on, every motion problem is forces first, then F=ma, then the constant acceleration formulae.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A force can be written in i, j form, and the resultant of several forces is their vector sum.
  • Newton's second law: resultant force = mass × acceleration, F=ma, with F in newtons, m in kg and a in ms−2.
  • The acceleration is in the direction of the resultant force.
By the end you can
Find the resultant of forces given in i, j form, and its magnitude and direction.
Use F=ma to find the acceleration of a body, or an unknown force or mass, together with the constant acceleration formulae.
Use F=ma for motion in a vertical line, such as a particle pulled upwards by a string, including its weight.

Forces add as vectors

The resultant of forces in i, j form is found by adding the i components and adding the j components. The resultant has the same effect as all the forces together.

Size and direction of a force

The magnitude of a force comes from its components by Pythagoras, and its direction from the tangent of the angle. A quick sketch shows which angle has been found.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

A resultant force causes an acceleration

Newton's second law says resultant force = mass × acceleration, F=ma. The acceleration is in the direction of the resultant force.

Moving vertically, the weight is a force too

When a body moves up or down, its weight mg is one of the forces in the resultant. Take the direction of the acceleration as positive.

Forces first, then motion

F=ma gives the acceleration from the forces. The constant acceleration formulae then give a speed, a distance or a time.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
A car of mass 800 kg starts from rest with a driving force of 2000 N against a resistance of 400 N: find its speed after 5 s.
Resultant first, then F=ma, then v=u+at.
Resolve forwards2000−400=800a
a=2ms−2
Find the speedv=0+2×5
=10ms−1
