Connected Particles

A car towing a trailer, a train pulling its carriages: two bodies joined together move as one, and F=ma used twice finds both the acceleration and the force in the connection. Next lesson the same idea finds the forces between stacked boxes and inside a lift.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Particles joined by a light inextensible string or a rod, such as a car towing a trailer, move with the same acceleration.
  • Treat the system as one body to find the acceleration, since the tension is internal, then one particle on its own to find the tension.
  • A rod or tow bar can push as well as pull: it is in thrust when the system decelerates.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Connected bodies share an acceleration

Two bodies joined by a light inextensible rope, or a rod, move together with the same acceleration. The tension pulls each body towards the other.

Whole system first, then one body

Treating the system as one body finds the acceleration, because the tension is internal and cancels. F=ma on one body alone then finds the tension.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
A car of mass 900 kg tows a trailer of mass 300 kg with a light horizontal rope, with a driving force of 1800 N and no resistance: find the acceleration and the tension.
One body of 1200 kg, then the trailer aloneonly the tension acts on it.
Whole system1800=1200a
a=1.5ms−2
Trailer aloneT=300×1.5
=450 N
WORKED EXAMPLE B
A car of mass 800 kg tows a trailer of mass 400 kg against resistances of 200 N and 100 N, with a driving force of 1500 N: find the tension.
The car's resistance is 200 N and the trailer's 100 N. Both resistances act on the whole system.
Whole system1500−300=1200a
a=1
Trailer aloneT−100=400×1
TensionT=500 N

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

A rod can push

A tow bar or rod can push as well as pull. When the system slows down, the bar is in thrust: it pushes the trailer back and the car forward.

When the connection breaks

When a tow rope breaks, each body moves on under its own forces only. Its speed at that moment is the initial speed for the next stage.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
