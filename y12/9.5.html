Graphs of Sine, Cosine and Tangent

The graphs of sinθ, cosθ and tanθ show how each ratio behaves for every angle, not only the angles of a right-angled triangle. Reading a period, a maximum or the number of solutions from them is what the transformations next lesson, and the equations of chapter 10, build on.

By the end you’ll know…
  • The graphs of y=sinθ and y=cosθ repeat every 360°, and their values lie between −1 and 1
  • The graph of y=cosθ is the graph of y=sinθ translated 90° to the left.
  • The graph of y=tanθ repeats every 180° and has vertical asymptotes at 90°, 270°, …, where tanθ is undefined.

02

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Sine and cosine are waves

The graphs of y=sinθ and y=cosθ repeat every 360°, and every value lies between −1 and 1
The cosine graph is the sine graph translated 90° to the left.

Tangent has asymptotes

The graph of y=tanθ repeats every 180° and passes through every value. It has no value at 90° and 270°, so the graph has vertical asymptotes there.

The pattern carries on

Over a wider interval each graph repeats the same shape to the left and to the right. Negative angles continue the pattern.

COSINE OVER NEGATIVE ANGLES
Sketch y=cosθ for −180°≤θ≤180°.
Mark the maximum (0°,1) and the minima (−180°,−1) and (180°,−1). The curve crosses the θ-axis at −90° and 90°. Join the points with a smooth wave.
TANGENT AND ITS ASYMPTOTES
Now sketch y=tanθ for −180°≤θ≤180°.
The asymptotes are at −90° and 90°, where tanθ has no value. The curve crosses the θ-axis at −180°, 0° and 180°. Each branch rises from left to right towards an asymptote.

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Read the period and the range

The period is the width of one repeat of the graph. For y=sinθ and y=cosθ the maximum is 1 and the minimum is −1

Count where y=k crosses

The solutions of sinθ=k are where the line y=k meets the curve. Count the crossings over the whole interval.

No crossing, no solution

A line above 1 or below −1 never meets the sine or cosine curve. Then the equation has no solutions.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
How many solutions has sinθ=0.3 for 0°≤θ≤720°?
The line y=0.3 meets each wave twice, and 720°÷360°=2 waves.
2×2=4 solutions
WORKED EXAMPLE B
How many solutions has tanθ=−2 for 0°≤θ≤540°?
Each branch of y=tanθ takes every value once, and 540°÷180°=3 branches.
3×1=3 solutions
YOUR TURN · c
How many solutions has cosθ=−0.6 for 0°≤θ≤360°?
The line y=−0.6 meets one cosine wave twice: 2 solutions
YOUR TURN · d
State the period and the maximum and minimum values of y=cosθ. How many solutions has cosθ=1 for 0°≤θ≤720°?
Period 360°, maximum 1, minimum −1
y=1 touches the curve at 0°, 360° and 720°: 3 solutions
