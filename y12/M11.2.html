Using Differentiation

Velocity is how fast displacement changes and acceleration is how fast velocity changes, so differentiating turns a displacement function into the velocity and the acceleration at any time. That lets you find the moments a body is at rest, which the next lesson uses to find its greatest displacement and speed.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Velocity is the rate of change of displacement, v=dsdt, and acceleration is the rate of change of velocity, dvdt.
  • Differentiate to go from displacement to velocity, and from velocity to acceleration.
  • The acceleration is the gradient of the velocity-time graph.
By the end you can
Differentiate a displacement given as a function of time to find the velocity and the acceleration at a given time.

Learn

Velocity is the gradient of s

Velocity is the rate of change of displacement, so v=dsdt. Differentiating s gives the gradient of the displacement-time graph at any time.

Differentiate again for the acceleration

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity, so a=dvdt. Differentiating s twice gives the acceleration, written d2sdt2

WORKED EXAMPLE A
Find the velocity when t=4 of a particle s metres from O after t seconds, where s =t3 −6t2 +5t.
Velocity is dsdtdifferentiate first, then substitute the time.
Differentiate sv=3t2−12t+5
Substitute the timev=48−48+5
=5ms−1
WORKED EXAMPLE B
Find the acceleration when t=4 of a particle s metres from O after t seconds, where s =t3 −6t2 +5t.
Acceleration is dvdtdifferentiate s twice, then substitute the time.
Differentiate sv=3t2−12t+5
Differentiate va=6t−12
Substitute the timea=24−12
=12ms−2

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

At rest means dsdt=0

To find when a body is at rest, differentiate s and set the velocity equal to zero. Each root in the valid times is a moment the s−t graph turns.

A given velocity or acceleration

Set the derivative equal to the given value, not s itself. Rearrange the equation to zero and solve it for t.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
Find when a particle with s =2t3 −9t2 +12t is at rest.
At rest means v=0: differentiate s, then set the velocity to zero.
Differentiate sv=6t2−18t+12
Set v to zero6(t−1)(t−2)=0
Timest=1 s
t=2 s
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Find when a particle with s =2t3 −9t2 +12t has velocity 12ms−1, for t>0
Set the velocity, not s, equal to 12
Differentiate sv=6t2−18t+12
Set v to 126t(t−3)=0
Timet=3 s, since t>0
