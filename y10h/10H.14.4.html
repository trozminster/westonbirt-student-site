Topic 10H.14 · Constructions and Circle Theorems · Lesson 4 of 6

Problem Solving with Circle Theorems

Exam circle questions are rarely one step. Chaining theorems, with a reason at each step, is what earns the marks.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A multi-step problem chains several theorems, each step feeding the next.
  • Every step needs its reason stated by name, since the reasoning is what carries the marks.
  • Circle theorems combine with the ordinary angle facts: triangles, straight lines, isosceles triangles and parallel lines.
  • On a goal-free diagram every angle that can be found is found, and the route to the target emerges from that.
By the end you can
G7Solve problems combining two or more circle theorems, giving reasons throughout.
G7Work from a goal-free diagram, finding every angle that can be found.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

One fact feeds the next

Each theorem finds one new angle. That angle unlocks the next step.

Name the reason, not the sum

Each step names the fact it uses. A sum on its own is not a reason.

Check the condition first

A theorem needs its condition shown in the diagram. A chord that looks central is not a diameter.

CENTRE, THEN CYCLIC
Find angle ADC.
Halve the centre angleangle ABC = 58°
Use opposite anglesangle ADC = 180 - 58
= 122°
SEMICIRCLE, THEN SAME SEGMENT
Now find angle ADC.
Use the semicircleangle ACB = 90°
Use the triangle's sumangle ABC = 56°
Use the same segmentangle ADC = 56°
YOUR TURN · a
O is the centre and angle AOB = 130°. C is on the major arc and D on the minor arc. Find angle ADB.
Angle ACB = 65°: half the angle at the centre. Angle ADB = 180 - 65 = 115°: opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral
YOUR TURN · b
The tangent at T makes 40° with chord TA, and O is the centre. Find angle TOA.
Angle OTA = 90 - 40 = 50°: the tangent meets the radius at 90°. OT = OA, so angle TOA = 180 - 50 - 50 = 80°

Angle ADB = 180 - 90 - 33 = 57°

Angle OTA = 90 - 36 = 54°
Angle TOA = 180 - 54 - 54 = 72°

Angle OAB = (180 - 124) ÷ 2 = 28°

Angle BCD = 180 - 74 = 106°: co-interior angles

EXT

Angle AOB = 180 - 2x: OA = OB
Angle ACB = (180 - 2x) ÷ 2 = 90 - x

Learn

Find everything you can

A goal-free diagram asks for every angle that can be found. The route to any target appears along the way.

Mark each angle as you go

Each found angle is written on the diagram. Its reason goes in the working beside it.
