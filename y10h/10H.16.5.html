Finding Key Values from a Cumulative Frequency Graph

A cumulative frequency graph of a hundred values gives the median and the interquartile range in three readings. Two sets of readings then compare two whole groups.

By the end you’ll know…
  • On a cumulative frequency graph with total frequency n, the median is read at n2, the lower quartile at n4 and the upper quartile at 3n4
  • Each is read across from the cumulative frequency axis to the curve, then down to the value axis.
  • The interquartile range is the upper quartile minus the lower quartile, both read from the graph.
  • These are estimates, because the data are grouped and the curve is drawn between the plotted points.

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Read the same values from each graph

Each graph gives a median and an interquartile range. The two sets of readings are then compared.

Median for typical, IQR for consistency

The median compares the typical value. The interquartile range compares how consistent the groups are.

Say what it means

Each comparison names the group and what the numbers mean. 'Group B finished faster on average' is complete.

LONGER IS WORSE
Compare school X, on the graph, with school Y: median 52 and IQR 25 minutes.
Read X's valuesmedian≈61
IQR≈81−40=41
Compare the mediansX's took longer on average

HIGHER IS BETTER
Now compare test 1, on the graph, with test 2: median 58 and IQR 12 marks.
Read test 1's valuesmedian≈53
IQR≈63−42=21
