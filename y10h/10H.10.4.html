Converting Units Between Dimensions

Flooring is sold by the square metre, while plans are drawn in centimetres. Moving between them is a factor of 100, used two or three times over.

By the end you’ll know…
  • 1 cm =10 mm, 1m=100 cm and 1 km =1000 m.
  • Area has two length dimensions, so the conversion factor is squared: 1 cm² =102 mm² =100 mm² and 1 m² =1002 cm² =10000 cm².
  • Volume has three, so it is cubed: 1 cm³ =103 mm³ =1000 mm³ and 1 m³ =1003 cm³ =1000000 cm³.
  • 1 litre =1000 cm³.

Area converts by the square

A square metre is 100 cm by 100 cm. So 1 m² =10,000 cm², not 100 cm².

One metre is 100 cm, so a square metre is 100 cm by 100 cm. Its area is 100×100=10,000 cm², not 100 cm².

Volume converts by the cube

Volume has three lengths, so the factor is cubed. So 1 cm³ =1000 mm³.

Volume has three lengths, so the length factor is cubed. So 1 cm³ =103 mm³ =1000 mm³.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Each part of the unit converts

Each part of a compound unit converts on its own. In g/cm³, the volume part converts by a cube.

A compound unit divides one unit by another, and each part converts on its own. In g/cm³, the volume part converts by a cube.

A rate converts twice

A rate's volume part and time part both convert. One litre is 1000 cm³; one minute is 60 seconds.

A rate of flow has a volume part and a time part, and both convert. One litre is 1000 cm³, and one minute is 60 seconds.
