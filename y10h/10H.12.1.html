Translations and Symmetry

Every slide on a screen, from a game character to a scrolling map, is two numbers: across and up. Symmetry lets a designer finish a logo or a tile from half of it.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A translation is specified by a distance and a direction and is written as a column vector with the horizontal movement above the vertical.
  • A positive top number moves right and a negative one left; a positive bottom number moves up and a negative one down.
  • A translation preserves length and angle, so the image is congruent to the object and points the same way.
  • A line of symmetry is a line about which a shape reflects onto itself.
  • The order of rotational symmetry is the number of positions, within one full turn, in which a shape looks the same; every shape has order at least 1.
By the end you can
G3Translate a shape by a given column vector and write down the column vector of a translation shown on a grid.
G3Complete a figure so that it has a stated line of symmetry or a stated order of rotational symmetry.

A translation slides a shape

A translation moves every point the same distance in the same direction. The image is congruent and faces the same way.

Top across, bottom up or down

A column vector puts the move across on top, and up or down below. So 3-2 means 3 right and 2 down.

From a point to its own image

Read a vector from any point to its own image. Count across first, then up or down.

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

A line of symmetry

A line of symmetry reflects a shape exactly onto itself. A rectangle has two, through the middles of its sides.

The order of rotational symmetry

The order counts the positions in one full turn where a shape looks the same. Every shape has order at least 1.

Complete it by reflecting each corner

Each missing corner is a reflection of a given one. It is as far from the line, on the other side.

Complete it by turning

For order 2, the missing part is a half turn of the given part. Each corner goes straight through the centre.
