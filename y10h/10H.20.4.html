Averages from Histograms

Doctors' waiting times and journey times are often published as histograms. Estimating their mean and median turns the picture back into numbers worth comparing.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A histogram's averages are found from its frequencies, so each class's frequency = frequency density x class width comes first.
  • The estimated mean = the total of midpoint × frequency, divided by the total frequency.
  • The median is the value with half the total frequency below it: count the areas from the left, then take the matching fraction of the class that holds it.
  • The modal class is the class with the greatest frequency density, the tallest bar.
