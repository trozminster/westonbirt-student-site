Intersecting Chords Inside and Outside a Circle

An engineer can find the radius of a broken wheel from one chord and the height of its arc. It works because crossing chords always multiply to the same product.

By the end you’ll know…
  • When two chords AB and CD cross at a point X inside a circle, AX x XB = CX x XD.
  • When two chords AB and CD, extended, meet at a point X outside the circle, XA x XB = XC x XD, each length measured from X.
  • In both cases the two lines through X cut the circle into parts whose products are equal, because the chords form a pair of similar triangles.

Crossing chords multiply

When two chords cross inside a circle, the products of their parts are equal. Each product uses one chord's two parts.

Why the products match

The chords make two similar triangles, by angles in the same segment. Their sides in proportion give the equal products.

TWO CHORDS CROSSING
Find XD.
Multiply each chord's parts4 × 6 = 3 × XD
Divide by 3XD = 24 ÷ 3
= 8
THE UNKNOWN IN BOTH PARTS
Now find x.
Multiply each chord's parts8x = 4(x + 2)
Expand and collect4x = 8
x = 2
YOUR TURN · a
Chords AB and CD cross at X. AX = 6, XB = 4 and CX = 8: find XD.
6 × 4 = 8 × XD, so XD = 3
YOUR TURN · b
Two chords cross. One is cut into parts of 6 and 8, and the other into parts x and 3x. Find x.
6 × 8 = x × 3x, so 3x² = 48 and x² = 16. x = 4

Chords that meet outside

Two chords, extended, can meet at a point outside the circle. Their parts, measured from that point, still give equal products.

Measure from X

Every length in the rule starts at X. So the far length runs through the whole chord.

TWO LINES FROM X
Find CD.
Add on the chordXB = 4 + 5
= 9
Multiply from X4 × 9 = 3 × XD
XD = 12
Subtract XCCD = 12 - 3
= 9
FORMING A QUADRATIC
Now find x.
Multiply from Xx(x + 5) = 4 × 6
Rearrange to zerox² + 5x - 24 = 0
Factorise and reject -8(x + 8)(x - 3) = 0
x = 3
YOUR TURN · c
Two chords, extended, meet at X outside the circle. XA = 5, AB = 7 and XC = 6: find XD.
XB = 5 + 7 = 12. 5 × 12 = 6 × XD, so XD = 10
YOUR TURN · d
Two chords meet at X outside the circle, with XA = x, AB = 3, XC = 2 and CD = 7: find x.
XD = 9, so x(x + 3) = 18. x² + 3x - 18 = 0, so (x + 6)(x - 3) = 0 and x = 3
