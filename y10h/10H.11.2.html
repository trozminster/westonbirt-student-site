Calculating with Fractions

Recipes, plans and shares come in fractions, and an exact answer keeps them as fractions. Mixed numbers become improper fractions so that the fraction rules apply.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Mixed numbers are converted to improper fractions before multiplying or dividing.
  • Adding and subtracting mixed numbers can be done either by converting, or by handling the wholes and the parts separately.
  • Algebraic fractions with numerical denominators follow the same rules as numerical ones, so x3+x4 needs a common denominator of 12
  • The order of operations applies to fractions exactly as it does to integers.
By the end you can
G4Add, subtract, multiply and divide mixed numbers, converting to improper fractions where needed.
G5Solve contextual problems involving the multiplication and division of fractions and mixed numbers.

Adding needs equal-sized parts

Fractions add only over a common denominator. Improper fractions avoid borrowing.

Fractions add or subtract only when their denominators match. Making mixed numbers improper first avoids borrowing.

Improper before multiplying

Mixed numbers become improper before multiplying. Wholes and parts multiplied separately go wrong.

Mixed numbers become improper fractions before multiplying or dividing. Multiplying the wholes and the parts separately gives the wrong answer.

Divide by turning the divisor over

Dividing by a fraction multiplies by its reciprocal. Only the divisor turns over.

Dividing by a fraction is multiplying by its reciprocal. Only the divisor is turned over.

Learn

Of means multiply

A fraction of a quantity is a multiplication. So 34 of 223 kg is 34×83 kg.

A fraction of a quantity is that fraction multiplied by it. So 34 of 223 kg is 34×83 kg, which is 2 kg.

A fraction of what is left

A second fraction is often of what is left. The remainder comes first.

A second fraction is often a fraction of what is left, not of the whole. The remainder is found before the second fraction is taken.

How many fit is a division

How many fit is a division. So 320 m books on a 214 m shelf is 214÷320

The number of pieces that fit in a length is a division. So 320 m books on a 214 m shelf is 214÷320
