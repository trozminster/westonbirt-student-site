Direct Proportion Formulae

Many real formulas are proportions: a falling stone's distance grows with the square of the time. One known pair of values fixes k, and the formula then answers any question.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A proportion problem is solved in four steps: write the formula with k, substitute one known pair to find k, rewrite the formula with that value of k, then use it to find the unknown.
  • y proportional to x2 gives y=kx2, so doubling x multiplies y by 4; y proportional to x3 gives y=kx3, so doubling x multiplies y by 8; y proportional to the square root of x gives y=kx.
  • The power is worked out before multiplying by k: when y=3x2 and x=4, y=3×16=48, not 144

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

One pair fixes k

One known pair of values fixes k. The formula then gives every other pair.

The formula works both ways

The formula finds y from x. It also finds x from y, by solving an equation.

FIND THE OUTPUT
The cost C of a rope is directly proportional to its length L, and £10 buys 4 m of it. Find the cost of 7 m of rope.
Write the formulaC=kL
Find kk=10÷4
=2.5
Cost of 7 mC=2.5×7
=£17.50
FIND THE INPUT
Now a car's distance d is directly proportional to the time t, and it covers 150 km in 2 hours. How long does it take to cover 375 km?
Write the formulad=kt
Find kk=150÷2
=75
Time for 375 kmt=375÷75
=5 hours

Learn

Square the input first

In y=kx2 the input is squared before k multiplies it. Doubling x multiplies y by 4

Same steps for any power

A cube or a root follows the same four steps. Only the formula changes.

SQUARED
The distance d a stone falls is directly proportional to the square of the time t, and it falls 20 m in 2 seconds. How far does it fall in 5 seconds?
Write the formulad=kt2
Find kk=20÷22
=5
Distance after 5 secondsd=5×52
=125 m
CUBED
Now the mass M of a metal cube is directly proportional to the cube of its edge e, and a 2 cm cube has mass 40 g. Find the edge of a cube of mass 135 g.
Write the formulaM=ke3
Find kk=40÷23
=5
Edge of the cubee=135÷53
=3 cm
