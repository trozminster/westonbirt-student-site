Unit Fractional Indices

If 2 cubed is 8, what power of 8 gives back 2? The index laws answer that question, and the answer turns every root into a power.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A power of 1n means the nth root: a1/2 is the square root of a and a1/3 is the cube root, because (a1/n)n=an/n=a1=a.
  • So 251/2=5, 81/3=2 and 811/4=3: the denominator of the index names the root.
  • The root of a fraction is the root of its numerator over the root of its denominator: (49)1/2 =23
  • A root can be written as a power: the cube root of x is x1/3.
By the end you can
G6Evaluate a whole number raised to a power 1n by taking the nth root.
G7Evaluate a fraction or a decimal raised to a power 1n, and write a root as a power, such as the cube root of x=x1/3.

A half power undoes squaring

Squaring 91/2 gives 9 by the power law. So 91/2 is the square root of 9, which is 3

By the power law, (91/2)2=91=9
So 91/2 is the number whose square is 9, which is the square root, 3

The denominator names the root

In a power of 1n, the n says which root to take. So 641/3 is the cube root of 64, not its square root.

In a power of 1n the n says which root to take: 2 for the square root, 3 for the cube root, 4 for the fourth. So 641/3 is the cube root of 64, which is 4, not its square root, 8

Root the top and the bottom

The root of a fraction is the root of each part. So (49)1/2 is 23

Rooting a fraction roots its numerator and its denominator separately, because the root of a product is the product of the roots. So (49)1/2 is 4 over 9, which is 23

A decimal becomes a fraction first

A decimal is easier to root as a fraction. So 0.091/2 is (9100)1/2, which is 310

A decimal is easier to root once it is written as a fraction over a power of 10
So 0.091/2 is (9100)1/2, which is 310, or 0.3
