Topic 10H.13 · Line Segments, Parallel and Perpendicular Lines · Lesson 6 of 7

Shapes, Bisectors and Tangents on a Grid

Design software checks right angles and equal sides from coordinates alone. Gradients test the angles, lengths test the sides, and a circle adds one more right angle.

By the end you’ll know…
  • In coordinates, two sides are parallel when their gradients are equal, meet at right angles when their gradients multiply to −1, and are equal when their lengths are equal.
  • The perpendicular bisector of AB passes through the midpoint of AB, and its gradient is the negative reciprocal of the gradient of AB.
  • The tangent to the circle x2+y2=r2 at a point P is perpendicular to the radius OP, where O is the origin.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Gradients test the angles

Equal gradients show parallel sides. Gradients that multiply to −1 show a right angle.

Lengths test the sides

Equal lengths show equal sides. Gradients alone cannot show it.

A RIGHT-ANGLED TRIANGLE
Show that triangle ABC, with A(1,2), B(3,6) and C(7,4), is right-angled at B.
Find the gradient of AB4÷2=2
Find the gradient of BC−2÷4=−12
Multiply the gradients2×(−12)=−1
So AB is perpendicular to BC, and the angle at B is a right angle
A PARALLELOGRAM
Now show that P(0,0), Q(4,1), R(5,4) and S(1,3) form a parallelogram.
Compare PQ and SRboth have gradient 14
Compare QR and PSboth have gradient 3
two pairs of parallel sides, so PQRS is a parallelogram

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Through the midpoint, at right angles

The perpendicular bisector of AB passes through its midpoint. Its gradient is the negative reciprocal of AB's.

A tangent is perpendicular to the radius

The tangent at P meets the radius OP at right angles. So its gradient is the negative reciprocal of OP's.

A PERPENDICULAR BISECTOR
Find the perpendicular bisector of A(−1,2) and B(5,6).
Find the midpoint(2,4)
Find the perpendicular gradientABhas46=23, so−32
Substitute the midpoint4=−3+c
y=−32x+7
A TANGENT TO A CIRCLE
Now find the tangent to x2+y2=20 at P(4,2).
Find the gradient of OP2÷4=12
Find the tangent's gradient−1÷12=−2
Substitute P2=−8+c
y=−2x+10
