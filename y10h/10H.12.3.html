Topic 10H.12 · Transformations and Enlargements · Lesson 3 of 6

Enlargement by a Positive Scale Factor

A projector, a photo print and a map all enlarge: every length is multiplied by the same number. The centre fixes where the enlarged image lands.

By the end you’ll know…
  • An enlargement is specified by a centre and a scale factor k.
  • Every length on the image is k times the matching length on the object, and the distance from the centre to an image point is k times the distance from the centre to its object point.
  • An enlargement preserves angles but not lengths, so the image is similar to the object and not congruent to it.
By the end you can
G3Enlarge a shape by a positive whole-number scale factor about a given centre, and about no stated centre.
G4Find the scale factor and the centre of an enlargement, given a shape and its image.

02

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Every length times k

An enlargement multiplies every length by the scale factor k. The angles stay the same: the image is similar, not congruent.

The centre fixes the place

Each image point is on the line from the centre through its object point. It is k times as far from the centre.

Count from the centre

Count the steps from the centre to a point, then multiply them by k. Multiply the coordinates only when the centre is (0, 0).

WORKED EXAMPLE A
Enlarge the flag by scale factor 3.
Lengthsthe stem 1 becomes 3 and the bar 1 becomes 3
Anglethe right angle at the top stays a right angle
Drawanywhere on the grid, since no centre is given
WORKED EXAMPLE B
Enlarge the flag by scale factor 2, centre (0, 1).
Countthe foot is 2 right and 1 up from the centre, so its image is 4 right and 2 up, at (4, 3)
Points(2, 3) to (4, 5) and (3, 3) to (6, 5)
Checkthe stem was 1 and is now 2
YOUR TURN · a
Enlarge the point (4, 3) by scale factor 3, centre (2, 1).
2 right and 2 up becomes 6 right and 6 up: (8, 7)
YOUR TURN · b
Enlarge the point (1, 5) by scale factor 2, centre (3, 2).
2 left and 3 up becomes 4 left and 6 up: (-1, 8)

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Image over object

The scale factor is an image length divided by its matching object length. Divide image by object, never subtract.

Lines meet at the centre

A line through each point and its image passes through the centre. Two such lines cross there.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
Find the scale factor from flag A to flag B. Match the stems: A's is 2 long and B's is 3 long. Divide image by object.
Scale factor3 ÷ 2 = 1.5
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Find the centre of enlargement, A to B.
Joineach foot to its image foot, and each top to its image top, and extend the lines back
Crossthe lines meet at (1, 0)
CheckB's foot is 3 times as far from (1, 0) as A's, and 3 is the scale factor
Centre(1, 0)
YOUR TURN · c
A side of 4 cm is 10 cm on an enlargement. Find the scale factor.
10 ÷ 4 = 2.5
YOUR TURN · d
Under scale factor 2, the point (3, 2) maps to (7, 4). Find the centre.
The step from (3, 2) to (7, 4), 4 right and 2 up, equals the step from the centre to (3, 2): (-1, 0)
