Quartiles and the Interquartile Range

Two classes can share a median and still have very different results. The interquartile range measures how spread out the middle half of the data is, and one extreme value cannot distort it.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Quartiles split ordered data into four equal parts, so a quarter of the values lie below the lower quartile and a quarter above the upper quartile.
  • For n ordered values, the lower quartile is the n+14th value, the median the n+12th value and the upper quartile the 3(n+1)4th value.
  • The interquartile range is the upper quartile minus the lower quartile: the spread of the middle half of the data.
  • Unlike the range, the interquartile range is not changed by one extreme value, so it is a more reliable measure of spread.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Compare an average and a spread

A fair comparison uses an average and a measure of spread. Here they are the median and the interquartile range.

A smaller IQR is more consistent

The interquartile range shows how consistent the results are. A smaller one means more consistent, not better.

Say what it means

Each statement says what the difference means for the data. 'Class A scored higher on average' is complete.

VALUES GIVEN
Compare teams P and Q, with medians of 24 and 19 points and IQRs of 6 and 11 points.
Compare the medians24>19, so P scored more on average
Compare the IQRs6<11, so P was more consistent
FROM THE LISTS
Now compare the times of A: 12, 14, 15, 17, 19, 20, 25 and B: 10, 15, 18, 19, 20, 23, 30, in seconds.
Find A's valuesmedian 17, IQR 20−14=6
Find B's valuesmedian 19, IQR 23−15=8
A was faster on average and more consistent
YOUR TURN · c
At shop X the median wait is 8 minutes, with IQR 3 minutes. At shop Y the median is 6 minutes, with IQR 7 minutes. Compare the waiting times.
Shop Y's waits were shorter on average
Shop X's waits were more consistent
YOUR TURN · d
Compare the scores of P: 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 12, 15 and Q: 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14
P: median 9, IQR 12−6=6; Q: median 10, IQR 12−7=5
Q scored higher on average, and Q's scores were more consistent
