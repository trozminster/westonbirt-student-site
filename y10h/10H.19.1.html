Topic 10H.19 · Algebraic Direct and Inverse Proportion · Lesson 1 of 3

Proportion Notation and Graphs

Science writes proportion as formulas: a car's stopping distance grows with the square of its speed. A statement in words becomes a formula, and each formula has its own graph.

By the end you’ll know…
  • 'y is proportional to x' is written with the proportion symbol between y and x, and replacing the symbol with '= k' turns it into a formula: y = kx, where k is the constant of proportionality.
  • 'Directly proportional to x2' gives y=kx2; 'inversely proportional to x' gives y=kx; 'inversely proportional to the square of x' gives y=kx2
  • For positive x, the graph of y=kx is a straight line through the origin with gradient k, the graph of y=kx2 is a curve through the origin that gets steeper, and the graph of y=kx is a curve that falls and approaches both axes without meeting them.
By the end you can
G6Write a statement of proportion given in words, such as 'y is inversely proportional to the square of x', with the proportion symbol and then as a formula with a constant k.
G6Match the graphs of y=kx, y=kx2 and y=kx to the proportion each shows, and find k from a point on the graph.

From words to a formula

The symbol ∝ means 'is proportional to'. Replacing it with =k turns the statement into a formula.

Inversely puts it under k

Inversely proportional puts the quantity under k. Any power or root stays with its letter.

DIRECT, CUBED

Write the symbolp ∝ q3
Bring in kp=kq3

Write the symbolF ∝ 1d2
Bring in kF=kd2

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Direct is a line through the origin

The graph of y = kx is a straight line through the origin. Its gradient is k.

Squared curves upward

The graph of y=kx2 also starts at the origin. It curves upward, getting steeper.

Inverse never reaches the axes

The graph of y=kx falls, then flattens. It never meets either axis.
