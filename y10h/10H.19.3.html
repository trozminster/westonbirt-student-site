Inverse Proportion Formulae

Light, sound and gravity all weaken with the square of the distance from their source. Inverse proportion formulas model them, and k is found by multiplying, not dividing.

By the end you’ll know…
  • y inversely proportional to x gives y=kx, so xy=k for every pair of values and doubling x halves y.
  • y inversely proportional to x2 gives y=kx2, so x2y=k and doubling x divides y by 4; y inversely proportional to the square root of x gives y=kx.
  • k is found by multiplying, not dividing: if y=5 when x=4 and y=kx2, then k=5×16=80

Every pair multiplies to k

In y=kx, every pair of values multiplies to k. One pair gives k by multiplying.

Divide k to use it

Once k is known, k divided by x gives y. Doubling x halves y.

Square before dividing

In y=kx2 the input is squared before k is divided by it. Doubling x divides y by 4

k is still a product

For y=kx2, k is x2 multiplied by y. Square x first, then multiply.
