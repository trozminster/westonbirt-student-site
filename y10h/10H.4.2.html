Areas of Sectors and Segments

A slice of pizza, a sprinkler's spray and a windscreen wiper's sweep are each a piece of a circle cut off by two radii. Its area is the circle's area scaled by the fraction of a full turn that it takes.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A sector is the region between two radii and the arc joining them; an angle of theta at the centre makes it theta360 of the whole circle, so its area is theta360 x pi x r².
  • The two radii cut the circle into a minor sector and a major sector, whose angles add to 360 degrees, so the major sector's angle is 360 - theta.
  • A segment is the region between a chord and its arc; its area is the sector's area minus the area of the triangle formed by the two radii and the chord.
By the end you can
G6Calculate the area of a sector given its radius and angle, exactly in terms of pi or to a stated accuracy, including a major sector.
G7Calculate the area of a segment by subtracting the triangle from its sector, where the triangle is right-angled at the centre or its base and height are given.

A sector is a fraction of a turn

A sector's angle is a fraction of the full 360°. Its area is that same fraction of the circle's area.

The angle between a sector's two radii is part of the full 360° at the centre. The sector covers exactly that same fraction of the circle, so its area is θ360 of π × r².

Two radii make two sectors

Two radii cut the circle into two sectors. The major sector's angle is 360° minus the minor one.

Two radii split the circle into a minor sector and a major sector. Their angles share the full turn, so the major sector's angle is 360° minus the minor one.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

A segment is a sector minus a triangle

A chord cuts a segment off the circle. Its area is the sector's area minus the triangle's.

A chord joining the ends of the two radii cuts the sector into a triangle and a segment. So the segment's area is whatever the sector has left once the triangle is taken away.

A right angle gives the triangle's height

With a right angle at the centre, the two radii are the base and the height. So the triangle's area is ½ × r × r.

When the angle at the centre is 90°, one radius is the triangle's base and the other is its perpendicular height. So the triangle's area is ½ × r × r, and the segment is the quarter circle minus that.
A RIGHT ANGLE AT THE CENTRE
A sector has radius 10 cm and a right angle at the centre. Find the area of its segment to 1 d.p.
Find the sector14 × π × 10² = 25π
Find the triangle12 × 10 × 10 = 50
Take the triangle away25π - 50
Segment28.5 cm²
BASE AND HEIGHT GIVEN
