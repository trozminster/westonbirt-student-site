Multiplying and Dividing in Standard Form

Light travels 3×105 km every second, and a year is about 3.2×107 seconds. Standard form turns the distance light travels in a year into one short multiplication.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Multiplying in standard form multiplies the A values and adds the powers of 10, because the factors can be taken in any order: (3×104) ×(5×106) =15×1010 =1.5×1011
  • Dividing divides the A values and subtracts the powers: (2×103) ÷(8×10−2) =0.25×105 =2.5×104
  • A power of a number in standard form raises both parts: (3×104)2=9×108; to take a square root, first make the power of 10 even: 1.6×109 =16×108, whose square root is 4×104
  • The answer is rewritten at the end if A has left 1≤A<10

A power acts on both parts

A power or a root acts on both parts. A square root needs an even power of 10 first.

A power or a root of a product acts on every factor, as the index laws showed. So squaring 3×104 squares the 3 and the 104; a square root needs an even power of 10 first.

How many times larger

Comparing by how many times larger is a division. The larger quantity goes on top.

Comparing two quantities by how many times larger one is means dividing them. The larger goes on top, and the answer is a plain number with no unit.
