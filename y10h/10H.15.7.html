Plotting and Recognising Non-Linear Graphs

Not every relationship is a straight line or a parabola. Cubic and reciprocal graphs model volumes and shared costs, and each has a shape you can spot from its equation.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A table of values generates points for any equation, not only for linear ones.
  • A quadratic, highest power x2, gives a parabola, symmetrical about a vertical line through its turning point.
  • A cubic, highest power x3, gives an S-shape.
  • A reciprocal, with x in the denominator, gives a hyperbola in two separate pieces that never touch the axes.
  • Non-linear points are joined with a smooth curve, never with straight segments.
By the end you can

02

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

A table gives the points

Substituting each x into the equation gives its y. The same method plots any graph, not only lines.

Join the points smoothly

The points of a non-linear graph lie on a curve. They are joined smoothly, never with straight segments.

A reciprocal skips x=0

Dividing by 0 is impossible, so x=0 has no point. The two pieces never touch the axes.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
Complete a table for y=x3−4x from x=−3 to 3, and plot the graph.
x=−3 gives −27+12=−15
the rest give 0, 3, 0,−3, 0 and 15
The points are joined with one smooth S-shaped curve.
WORKED EXAMPLE B
Complete a table for y=6x at x=1, 2, 3, 6 and their negatives, and plot it.
x=1, 2, 3, 6 give y=6, 3, 2, 1
the negatives give −6,−3,−2,−1
There is no point at x=0, and each piece is joined smoothly.

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

A squared term makes a parabola

An x2 as the highest power gives a parabola. A positive x2 opens upwards, a negative one downwards.

A cubed term makes an S-shape

An x3 as the highest power gives an S-shape. It rises at one end and falls at the other.

An x in the denominator makes two pieces

With x in the denominator, the graph is a reciprocal. Its two pieces never touch the axes.

READ THE HIGHEST POWER

Find the highest powerx3, so a cubic
Read the sign−2x3, so it falls from left to right
