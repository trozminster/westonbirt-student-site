Drawing and Measuring Tangents

A speedometer shows speed at one instant, not across a whole journey. On a curved graph, a straight line drawn at one point measures that instant.

By the end you’ll know…
  • The gradient of a curve changes from point to point, so a rate of change on a curve belongs to one instant.
  • A tangent touches the curve at one point and has the same gradient as the curve there.
  • The tangent's gradient is its rise divided by its run, read from the axis scales, and a large triangle gives a more accurate reading.
  • A chord joins two points on the curve, and its gradient is the average rate of change between them, not the rate at a point.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

The gradient changes along a curve

A curve's steepness changes from point to point. A tangent touches the curve and shares its gradient there.

Rise over run on the tangent

The gradient is the tangent's rise over its run, on the axis scales. A large triangle reads it most accurately.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
The curve shows a plant's height. Estimate its growth rate at 4 weeks.
Draw the line touching the curve at 4 weeks only, through (2,4) and (6,12). On it, a triangle 4 across and 8 up: 8÷4=2 cm per week.
WORKED EXAMPLE B
The curve shows a drink cooling. Estimate its rate of change at 2 minutes.
The tangent at 2 minutes passes through (0,70) and (4,30). It falls 40 over a run of 4, so the gradient is −10: the drink cools at 10 °C per minute.

A chord gives an average rate

A chord joins two points on the curve. Its gradient is the average rate of change between them.

Closer points, closer gradients

As the second point moves closer, the chord's gradient nears the tangent's. At x=1 on y=x2, the chords close in on 2

AN AVERAGE RATE
A ball rolls d=2t2 metres in t seconds. Find its average speed from t=1 to t=3
Find both distancesd=2
d=18
Average speed(18−2)÷(3−1)=8 m/s
AVERAGE AGAINST INSTANT
Now the tangent to d=2t2 at t=3 passes through (2,6) and (4,30). Compare the speed at t=3 with that average.
Speed now24÷2=12 m/s
Average speed8 m/s
