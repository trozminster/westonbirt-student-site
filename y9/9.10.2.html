Converting Compound Units

A sprinter at 10 m/s and a car at 36 km/h are going at exactly the same speed. Today is how to see that: a compound unit converts one part at a time.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A compound unit converts by converting each of its parts in turn.
  • ms to kmh multiplies by 3600 to turn seconds into hours and divides by 1000 to turn metres into kilometres, so overall it multiplies by 3.6.
  • The unit on the bottom converts the opposite way round to the one on top.
  • Every quantity must be in the units the answer is wanted in before any calculation starts.

Learn

One part at a time

A compound unit converts by converting each part in turn. The unit on the bottom converts the opposite way to the one on top.

The shortcut

Together those steps multiply by 3.6. So ms to kmh is × 3.6, and kmh to ms is ÷ 3.6.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
Convert 20 m/s to kmh.
Convert to per hour20 × 3600 = 72000 m/h
Divide by 1000speed = 72000 ÷ 1000
Speed in km/h= 72 km/h
WORKED EXAMPLE B
Convert 90 km/h to ms.
Convert to per second90 ÷ 3600 = 0.025 km/s
Speed in m/s0.025 × 1000 = 25 m/s
Check25 × 3.6 = 90
YOUR TURN · a
Convert 15 m/s to kmh.
15 × 3.6 = 54 km/h
YOUR TURN · b
Convert 108 km/h to ms.
108 ÷ 3.6 = 30 m/s
YOUR TURN · c
Convert 1.2 g/cm³ to kgm³.
1.2 g = 0.0012 kg and 1 cm³ = 0.000001 m³, so 1200 kg/m³.
YOUR TURN · d
Which is faster, a cheetah at 30 m/s or a car at 100 km/h?
30 × 3.6 = 108 km/h, so the cheetah.

Convert before you calculate

Every quantity must be in the units the answer needs before any calculation starts. Mixed units give a wrong answer that looks right.

Choose the unit to convert

Convert the quantity that makes the arithmetic easiest. The answer's own units decide what everything else must become.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
How long does 400 m take at 5 km/h?
Convert to km400 m = 0.4 km
Divide distance by speedtime = 0.4 ÷ 5
= 0.08 hours
Time in minutes0.08 × 60 = 4.8 minutes
WORKED EXAMPLE D

= 4500 m
