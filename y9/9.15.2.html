Dividing into a Ratio

A ratio can share a quantity out, not just compare two amounts. Today is about turning a ratio into an actual number of items for each share.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A ratio can be read as a total number of equal parts to be shared out.
  • The total divided by the number of parts gives the value of one part.
  • Each share is then that part value multiplied by its own number of parts.
  • The difference between two shares in a:b is (a - b) parts, so a given difference also gives the value of one part.
By the end you can
G4Divide a quantity into a given ratio with two or three parts.
G5Solve problems where the difference between two shares is given.

Parts and shares

A ratio shares a total into equal parts. Total ÷ number of parts = the value of one part.

A ratio can be read as a total number of equal parts to be shared out. The total divided by the number of parts gives the value of one part.

Building each share

Add every part of the ratio to find the total parts. Each share is one part × its own number of parts.

Each share is that one-part value multiplied by its own number of parts. Every part in the ratio must be added together to find the total number of parts.

When the difference is given

Sometimes the difference between two shares is given, not the total. In a:b, the difference is the larger part minus the smaller. That also gives the value of one part.

Sometimes a problem gives the difference between two shares rather than the total. The difference between two shares in a:b is the larger part minus the smaller, so that difference also gives the value of one part.

Same method, different starting point

Once one part is known, every share and the total follow. Only the starting point changes.

Once the value of one part is known, every share and the total follow exactly as before. The only change is where that one-part value comes from.
DIFFERENCE BETWEEN AMOUNTS
Two amounts are in the ratio 3:7. The larger is 24 more than the smaller.
Find the difference in parts7 - 3 = 4 The 24 is the difference, so it spans the 4 parts between them.
Find one part24 ÷ 4 = 6
The two amounts3 × 6 = 18
7 × 6 = 42
