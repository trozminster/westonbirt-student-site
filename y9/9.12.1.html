Cancelling Algebraic Fractions

A fraction with letters in it cancels by exactly the same rule as 1218 - find what the numerator and denominator share, then divide it out.

By the end you’ll know…
  • An algebraic fraction cancels by common factors in the numerator and denominator, exactly as a numerical one does.
  • Only factors cancel, never individual terms of a sum.
  • Powers of a letter cancel by subtracting indices, so x⁵ ÷ x² = x³.
  • Coefficients cancel by dividing both by their HCF.
  • Different letters cancel independently of one another.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Only factors cancel

A fraction simplifies by dividing top and bottom by a common factor. It must multiply the whole top and the whole bottom.

A fraction simplifies by dividing numerator and denominator by a common factor. A letter or number can only be removed if it multiplies the whole of both the top and the bottom.

Powers cancel by subtracting

x⁵ over x² leaves x³: subtract the powers. Two x's on top pair off with the two below.

x⁵ over x² leaves x⁵⁻² = x³, because two of the five x's on top pair off with the two x's on the bottom.

Write the expression12x³8x

= 3x²2 Then cancel x: x³ ÷ x = x².

Write the expression18x⁵24x²
Cancel the numbers= 3x⁵4x²
= 3x³4 Then cancel x²: x⁵ ÷ x² = x³.

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Cancel each letter separately

Cancel each letter's powers on their own. Cancel the coefficients by their HCF. e.g. 6a³b8ab⁴ = 3a²4b³.

A fraction with several letters cancels each letter's powers on its own, and cancels the coefficients by their HCF too.

A different letter is independent

Each letter cancels by its own rule. What happens to one letter never affects another.

Cancelling one letter follows its own rule and does not depend on what happens to any other letter in the fraction.
WORKED EXAMPLE C
Simplify 10a²b15ab².
Write the expression10a²b15ab²
Cancel the numbers= 2a²b3ab²
Cancel the a terms= 2ab3b²
= 2a3b Then cancel b: b on top and b² below leave 1b.
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Simplify 14x³y²z21xyz².
Write the expression14x³y²z21xyz²
Cancel the numbers= 2x³y²z3xyz²
Cancel the x terms= 2x²y²z3yz²
Cancel the y terms= 2x²yz3z²
= 2x²y3z Then cancel z: z on top and z² below leave 1z.
