Multiplying and Dividing Fractions

Multiplying and dividing algebraic fractions follow the same two rules as numerical ones: multiply straight across, and turn a division into a multiplication by flipping the second fraction.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Algebraic fractions multiply numerator by numerator and denominator by denominator.
  • Dividing means multiplying by the reciprocal, which is that fraction inverted.
  • Cancelling common factors before multiplying keeps the expressions small.
  • Cancelling can be done across the two fractions, not only within one of them.
  • The result is fully simplified only when no common factor is left.
By the end you can

Flip and multiply

Dividing by a fraction means multiplying by its reciprocal. The reciprocal is the fraction turned upside down.

Dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal, the fraction turned upside down.
