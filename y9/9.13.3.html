Volume Units and Reverse

Converting a volume unit is not the same as converting a length, because all three dimensions change, not just one.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Converting a length unit changes a volume unit by the cube of that factor: 1 m³ = 1000000 cm³ and 1 cm³ = 1000 mm³.
  • This is because all three dimensions are converted, not just one.
  • An area unit converts by the square of the factor, for the same reason.
  • 1 cm³ holds 1 ml, so 1000 cm³ is 1 litre.
  • A missing dimension is found by dividing the volume, or by rearranging the surface area formula, before solving.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Divide to find a missing dimension

Divide the volume by the product of the two known dimensions. The answer is the third dimension.

If the volume and two dimensions of a cuboid are known, the third is found by dividing the volume by the product of the other two.

Rearrange the surface area formula

Substitute the two known dimensions into 2(lw + lh + wh). Then solve the equation for the unknown.

Finding a dimension from a surface area means solving 2(lw + lh + wh) for the unknown, which needs the other two dimensions substituted first.

35h = 210
Divide both sides by 35h = 6 cm
