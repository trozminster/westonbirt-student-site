Pressure and Rates

A drawing pin goes into a board and a thumb does not, though the thumb pushes just as hard. The difference is the area the force is spread over, which is what pressure measures.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Pressure is force divided by area, measured in Nm2 or Ncm2.
  • It follows the same triangular relationship as speed and density, so force = pressure x area and area = force / pressure.
  • Any 'per' quantity works this way: a rate is one measure divided by another.
  • A multi-step problem finds an intermediate quantity first, keeping it exact until the end.

02

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Pressure is force per area

Pressure is force divided by area, in Nm² or Ncm². The same force on a smaller area gives a bigger pressure.

Any per quantity

A rate is one measure divided by another, like pounds per litre. It rearranges exactly as speed, density and pressure do.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
A force of 60 N acts on an area of 3 m². Find the pressure.
Divide force by areapressure = 60 ÷ 3
Pressure= 20 N/m²
WORKED EXAMPLE B
A pressure of 15 N/cm² acts on 8 cm². Find the force.
Rearrangeforce = pressure × area
Substitute= 15 × 8
Force= 120 N
YOUR TURN · a
A force of 200 N acts on 4 m². Find the pressure.
200 ÷ 4 = 50 N/m²
YOUR TURN · b
A pressure of 25 N/m² comes from a force of 150 N. Find the area.
area = force ÷ pressure = 150 ÷ 25 = 6 m²
YOUR TURN · c
A box of weight 90 N has a base 30 cm by 20 cm. Find the pressure in Ncm².
area = 600 cm², so 90 ÷ 600 = 0.15 N/cm²
YOUR TURN · d
The same box is turned onto a face 10 cm by 20 cm. What happens to the pressure?
area = 200 cm², so 90 ÷ 200 = 0.45 N/cm²: three times as much.

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Several steps

A multi-step problem finds an intermediate quantity first, such as an area or a mass. Keep it exact until the end.

Area units

1 m² is 100 cm by 100 cm, which is 10000 cm². Convert an area with the square of the length factor.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
A crate of weight 600 N stands on a base 1.2 m by 0.5 m. Find the pressure.
Find the area1.2 × 0.5 = 0.6 m²
Divide force by areapressure = 600 ÷ 0.6
Pressure= 1000 N/m²
WORKED EXAMPLE D
A car uses 6 litres of fuel per 100 km. Fuel costs £1.50 per litre. Find the cost of 250 km.
Find the fuel per km6 ÷ 100 = 0.06 litres
Multiply by 2500.06 × 250 = 15 litres
Multiply by the pricecost = 15 × 1.50
Cost of 250 km= £22.50
YOUR TURN · e
A table of weight 400 N stands on four legs, each with area 25 cm². Find the pressure under the legs in Ncm².
area = 4 × 25 = 100 cm², so 400 ÷ 100 = 4 N/cm²
YOUR TURN · f
A printer prints 18 pages per minute. How long, in minutes and seconds, does it take to print 45 pages?
45 ÷ 18 = 2.5 minutes = 2 minutes 30 seconds
YOUR TURN · g
Convert a pressure of 3 N/cm² to Nm².
1 m² = 10000 cm², so 3 × 10000 = 30000 N/m²
YOUR TURN · h
A pump fills a 1500 litre tank in 12 minutes. A second pump works twice as fast. How long do both together take?
125 + 250 = 375 litres per minute, so 1500 ÷ 375 = 4 minutes.
