Topic 9.2 · Linear Graphs and y = mx + c · Lesson 6 of 7

Rearranging into y = mx + c

Not every linear equation arrives already in the friendly y = mx + c form - most start tangled up, with x and y mixed together and sometimes a coefficient in front of y itself. Today you'll learn to untangle any linear equation into that form using the same balancing moves as solving an equation, so its gradient and intercept become visible.

By the end you’ll know…
  • The gradient and intercept can only be read off directly when the equation is in the form y = mx + c.
  • Any linear equation can be rearranged into that form, using the same inverse operations as solving.
  • Dividing through by the coefficient of y has to be applied to every term.
  • A negative coefficient of x makes m negative, which is easily lost during the rearrangement.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Why the form matters

Read m and c only from the form y = mx + c. Rearrange any linear equation with inverse operations on both sides.

The gradient and intercept can only be read off directly once an equation is written in the form y = mx + c - reading them from any other arrangement risks reading the wrong numbers. Any linear equation can be rearranged into that form using the same inverse operations used to solve an equation: adding, subtracting, multiplying or dividing both sides by the same thing.

Dividing every term

To isolate y, divide every term by its coefficient. That includes the constant term.

When the coefficient of y is not 1, dividing through to isolate y means dividing every single term on both sides by that coefficient, including any constant term - not just the terms that contain x or y.
WORKED EXAMPLE A
Rearrange 4x + y = 8 into the form y = mx + c.
Write the equation4x + y = 8
Subtract 4x from both sidesy = 8 - 4x
= -4x + 8 In the order y = mx + c: subtracting 4x leaves -4x, a negative gradient.
WORKED EXAMPLE B
Rearrange 2y + 4x = 8 into the form y = mx + c.
Write the equation2y + 4x = 8
Subtract 4x from both sides2y = 8 - 4x
Divide every term by 2y = 4 - 2x Divide the constant as well as the x-term.
= -2x + 4
YOUR TURN · a
Rearrange 2x + y = 5 into the form y = mx + c.
y = -2x + 5
YOUR TURN · b
Rearrange 3x + y = 6 into the form y = mx + c, then rearrange 2y + 6x = 10 into the form y = mx + c. (Hint: move the x-term first, then divide every term by the coefficient of y.)
3x + y = 6 gives y = -3x + 6. And 2y + 6x = 10 gives 2y = 10 - 6x, so y = -3x + 5.
YOUR TURN · c
Rearrange 2y - 4x = 6 into y = mx + c.
2y = 4x + 6, so y = 2x + 3.
YOUR TURN · d
Rearrange 3y - 6x = 12 into the form y = mx + c. (This one has a minus sign in front of the x-term already on the same side as y - take care with the sign as you rearrange, and remember to divide every term by 3.)
3y - 6x = 12 gives 3y = 6x + 12, so y = 2x + 4.

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Both numbers, then the equation

Find both the gradient and the intercept from the graph. Then combine them into one equation, y = mx + c.

Interpreting a pre-drawn graph means finding both its gradient and its intercept, and then combining them into a single equation in the form y = mx + c - stating the two values separately isn't the same as stating the equation itself.

Reading each one off

Read the intercept where the line crosses the y-axis. Find the gradient from rise and run between two marked points.

The intercept is read directly from where the line crosses the y-axis; the gradient is found from the rise and run between two clearly-marked points on the line.
WORKED EXAMPLE C
Find the gradient and the y-intercept of this line. The line crosses the y-axis at (0, -4) and passes through (2, 0).
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Using the values just found, write the full equation of the line.
YOUR TURN · e
A pre-drawn line crosses the y-axis at (0, 3) and passes through (4, -5). Find its gradient and intercept, then write its full equation. (Hint: once you've found both values, write them together as y = mx + c - don't leave them as two separate answers.)
gradient = (-5 - 3) ÷ (4 - 0) = -2, intercept = 3, so y = -2x + 3.
YOUR TURN · f
A line has m = -2 and c = 1. Write its equation.
y = -2x + 1
YOUR TURN · g
A pre-drawn line crosses the y-axis at (0, -1) and passes through (-3, 5), note the second point has a negative x. Find its gradient and intercept, then write its full equation.
gradient = (5 - (-1)) ÷ (-3 - 0) = -2, intercept = -1, so y = -2x - 1.
