Spheres, Pyramids, Surface Area

A cylinder's own net gives its surface area formula; the sphere and cone formulae are given in the exam, a hemisphere is half a sphere, and the pyramid's 13 × base × height must be remembered, so the skill is choosing and substituting correctly.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A cylinder's net is two circles and a rectangle whose width is the circumference, 2×π×r.
  • So its total surface area is 2×π×r2 +2×π×r×h.
  • A prism's surface area is its two cross-sections plus the rectangle formed by the perimeter × length.
  • The sphere and cone formulae are given in the exam; a hemisphere is half a sphere, and the pyramid's 13 × base × height must be remembered, so what matters is choosing the right one and identifying the correct radius or height.
  • A hemisphere's surface area includes its flat circular face as well as the curved half.

A cylinder's net gives its formula

A cylinder's net is two circles and a rectangle. The rectangle's width is the circumference, 2πr. So surface area =2πr2 +2πrh.

A cylinder's net is two circles and a rectangle whose width is the circumference, 2×π×r, giving total surface area 2×π×r2 +2×π×r×h.

A prism's surface area sums its parts

A prism's surface area is its two ends plus one rectangle. The rectangle is the cross-section's perimeter × length. e.g. ends 6 cm², perimeter 12 cm, length 10 cm.

A prism's surface area is its two cross-sections plus the rectangle formed by the cross-section's perimeter × length.

Choosing the formula

The sphere and cone formulae are given in the exam. A hemisphere is half a sphere. Remember the pyramid: 13 × base × height. Choose the right one and the correct radius or height.

The sphere and cone formulae are given in the exam; a hemisphere is half a sphere, and the pyramid's 13 × base × height must be remembered. What matters is choosing the right one and identifying the correct radius or height.

A hemisphere keeps its flat face

A hemisphere's surface area includes its flat circular face as well as its curved half.
