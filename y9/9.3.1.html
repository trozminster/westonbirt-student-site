Topic 9.3 · Angles in Polygons · Lesson 1 of 4

Polygons and Exterior Angles

Stop signs are octagons, footballs are covered in pentagons and hexagons, and floor tiles rely on knowing exactly which polygons fit together - all of it starts with being able to name a shape correctly and see the hidden 360° hiding in every one of them, however irregular it looks.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A polygon is a closed shape with straight sides, named by how many it has: pentagon 5, hexagon 6, heptagon 7, octagon 8, nonagon 9, decagon 10
  • A regular polygon has all sides equal and all angles equal; an irregular one does not.
  • The exterior angles of any polygon sum to 360°, because walking once round the shape is one full turn.
  • In a regular polygon every exterior angle is the same, so each one is 360÷n.
  • The number of sides follows from a given exterior angle, as n=360 ÷ exterior angle.

Naming polygons

A polygon is a closed shape with straight sides. Polygons are named by their number of sides. Pentagon 5, hexagon 6, heptagon 7, octagon 8, nonagon 9, decagon 10

A polygon is a closed shape made only of straight sides. Polygons are named by how many sides they have: pentagon 5, hexagon 6, heptagon 7, octagon 8, nonagon 9, decagon 10

Regular vs irregular

A regular polygon has all sides equal AND all angles equal. An irregular polygon does not.

A regular polygon has all sides equal AND all angles equal. An irregular polygon does not - it can still be closed with straight sides, but the sides and/or angles are not all the same.

Exterior angles

An exterior angle is between one side and the next side extended. Walking round any polygon turns you through one full turn. So exterior angles always sum to 360°.

The exterior angle at a vertex is the angle between one side and the extension of the next side. If you walk once all the way round the outside of ANY polygon - regular or irregular - you turn through exactly one full turn. So the exterior angles of any polygon always sum to 360°.

Exterior angle of a regular polygon

A regular polygon's n exterior angles are equal and sum to 360°. So each exterior angle =360÷n.

In a regular polygon, all n exterior angles are equal (since all sides and angles are equal), and they still sum to 360°. So each exterior angle =360÷n.

Working backwards to find n

Rearranged, n=360 ÷ exterior angle. Divide 360 by one exterior angle to find the number of sides.

Because each exterior angle equals 360n, the number of sides can be found by rearranging: n=360 ÷ exterior angle. Given one exterior angle of a regular polygon, divide 360 by it to find how many sides the polygon has.
WORKED EXAMPLE C
Find the exterior angle of a regular nonagon.
A nonagon has 9 sides. All 9 exterior angles are equal and they sum to 360°, so share 360° between 9
Exterior angle360÷9=40°
WORKED EXAMPLE D
A regular polygon has an exterior angle of 24°. Find the number of sides.
Each exterior angle is 360n, so the number of sides is n=360 ÷ exterior angle.
Divide 360 by 24n=36024
Number of sides=15
