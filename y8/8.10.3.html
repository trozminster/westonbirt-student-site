Topic 8.10 · Changing the Subject · Lesson 3 of 5

Multi-Step Rearranging

Most real formulae, like the one converting Celsius to Fahrenheit, are built in several steps. Undoing those steps in reverse order turns any of them round completely.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Multi-step rearrangement undoes operations in reverse order, exactly as when solving an equation.
  • The operation furthest from the wanted subject is undone first.
  • A fraction is cleared by multiplying both sides by its denominator before anything else is moved.
  • Where the subject sits inside a bracket, the bracket is expanded or divided out before the subject is isolated.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Undo in reverse order

A multi-step rearrangement undoes the operations in the reverse of the order they were applied. The operation furthest from the wanted subject is undone first.

A bracket around the subject

If the subject is inside a bracket, clear the bracket first. Only then move the number inside it.

Where the subject sits inside a bracket, the bracket is divided out or expanded before the subject is isolated. Only then is the number inside the bracket moved.
WORKED EXAMPLE A
Make x the subject of y = 3x + 5.
Subtract 5 from both sidesy - 5 = 3x
Divide by 3x = y - 53
WORKED EXAMPLE B
Make x the subject of A = 2(x + 3).
Divide both sides by 2A2 = x + 3
Subtract 3x = A2 - 3
YOUR TURN · a
Make t the subject of v = u + at.
v - u = at, so t = v - ua
YOUR TURN · b
Make x the subject of y = 5 - 2x.
2x = 5 - y, so x = 5 - y2

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Clear the fraction first

Clear a fraction first: multiply both sides by its denominator. Multiply every term, not only the fraction.

A fraction is cleared by multiplying both sides by its denominator before anything else is moved. Every term on both sides is multiplied, not only the fraction.

A subject in the denominator

If the subject is in a denominator, multiply both sides by it. Then free it in the usual way.

When the subject is in a denominator, multiplying both sides by it brings it onto the top line. It is then freed in the usual way.
WORKED EXAMPLE C
Make x the subject of y = x4 + 3.
Multiply every term by 44y = x + 12
Subtract 12x = 4y - 12
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Make t the subject of s = dt.
Multiply both sides by tst = d
Divide by st = ds
YOUR TURN · c
