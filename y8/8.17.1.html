Averages Recap

A single number can sum up a whole set of data, but which number, and what does it hide? Today we recap the averages and use them to compare two groups fairly.

By the end you’ll know…
  • The mean is the total of the values divided by how many there are.
  • The median is the middle value once the data is ordered; with an even count it is halfway between the middle two.
  • The mode is the most frequent value, and there may be more than one or none at all.
  • The range is the largest value minus the smallest, and measures spread rather than a typical value.
  • A stem-and-leaf diagram keeps every original value in order, so all four measures can be read straight from it.
By the end you can
G3Find the mean, median, mode and range of listed data.
G4Find averages from a stem-and-leaf diagram, and compare two data sets using an average and the range.

Three averages and a spread

The mean shares the total equally. The median is the middle value once ordered, the mode the most common. The range, largest minus smallest, measures spread.

The mean shares the total equally, the median is the middle value once ordered, and the mode is the most common value. The range, largest minus smallest, measures spread rather than a typical value.

The middle of an even count

An even number of values has two middle values. The median is halfway between them. Order the data first.

With an even number of values there are two middle values, and the median is halfway between them. The data must be in order first.

A stem-and-leaf keeps every value

Stem 3 with leaf 4 means 34: tens, then units. Every value stays, in order. So all four measures can be read straight off.

The stem gives the tens and each leaf a unit, so 3 | 4 means 34. Every value stays, in order, so all four measures can be read straight off.

Comparing two sets

An average says which set is typically higher. The range says which is more consistent. Write each comparison about the context, not just the numbers.

An average says which set is typically higher, and the range says which is more consistent. Each comparison is written about the context, not just the numbers.

A stem-and-leaf shows 3 | 1 5 7 and 4 | 2 2 6, with 3 | 1 meaning 31. Find the mode and the median.
