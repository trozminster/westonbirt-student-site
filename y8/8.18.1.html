Translations and Reflections

A shape can be slid or flipped without changing its size or shape at all. Today we describe exactly how far it slides, and exactly where it lands when flipped.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A translation slides a shape without turning or resizing it, so the image is identical to the original.
  • A column vector gives the movement: right or left on top, up or down below, with negatives for left and down.
  • In a reflection every point moves to the same perpendicular distance on the other side of the mirror line.
  • A point lying on the mirror line does not move.
  • Reflecting in y=x swaps a point's coordinates; reflecting in y=−x swaps them and changes both signs.
By the end you can

G5Reflect a shape in the lines x=a, y=b, y=x and y=−x.

A translation slides

A translation slides a shape without turning or resizing it. Every point moves the same distance in the same direction.

A translation slides a shape without turning or resizing it, so the image is identical to the original. Every point moves the same distance in the same direction.

The column vector

Top number: right (+) or left (-). Bottom number: up (+) or down (-). So (4−1) means 4 right and 1 down.

A column vector gives the movement: the top number is right (+) or left (-), the bottom number up (+) or down (-). So (4−1) means 4 right and 1 down.

As far behind the mirror

Each point moves to the same perpendicular distance behind the mirror line. A point on the mirror line does not move.

In a reflection every point moves to the same perpendicular distance on the other side of the mirror line. A point on the mirror line does not move.

The lines y=x and y=−x

Reflecting in y=x swaps the coordinates: (1,3) goes to (3,1). Reflecting in y=−x also changes both signs: (1,3) goes to (−3,−1).

Reflecting in y=x swaps a point's coordinates, so (1,3) goes to (3,1). Reflecting in y=−x swaps them and changes both signs, so (1,3) goes to (−3,−1).
