Exit ticket · 8.5.2
Explain the two steps of the unitary method, and explain why an exchange rate works in both directions.
Step 1: divide to find the value of one unit. Step 2: multiply by the number of units wanted. An exchange rate works both ways because it's just a ratio, and any ratio can be scaled or reversed to convert in either direction.
