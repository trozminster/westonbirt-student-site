Distance-Time Graphs

A journey can be told as a graph: every stage, every stop and every return shows up as a straight section. Today we read those stories and draw our own.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A distance-time graph shows distance from a starting point on the vertical axis against time on the horizontal.
  • A horizontal line means stationary, since time passes but distance does not change.
  • A steeper line means a faster speed.
  • A line returning to the horizontal axis means travelling back to the start.
  • Each straight section is one stage of the journey, travelled at a constant speed.

What the axes say

A distance-time graph plots distance from the start against time. Each straight section is one stage of the journey, travelled at a constant speed.

Flat, steep and back

A horizontal section means the object is stationary. A steeper section means a faster speed. A section back down to zero is a return to the start.

A horizontal section means stationary, since time passes but distance does not change. A steeper section is a faster speed, and a section back down to zero is a return to the start.

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Each stage starts where the last ended

Draw a journey one stage at a time. Each line starts where the last one ended. Distance is always measured from the start.

A journey is drawn one stage at a time, each line starting where the previous one ended. Distance is always measured from the start, not from the last stop.

Choose the scales first

Choose axes that reach the end time and the furthest distance. Then a stop is a horizontal line.

The time axis must reach the end of the journey and the distance axis its furthest point. A stop is then a horizontal line, and a return comes back down to zero.

Not quite. Sloping down means heading home. Stopping is flat.
Yes. Stopped means the distance stays at 8 km: a flat line, 20 minutes long.
Not quite. Time goes across, not up. Stopping is flat for 20 minutes.
Not quite. The cyclist is still 8 km away, so the line stays at 8 km.
On a distance-time graph, what does a steeper section show?
Yes. Steeper means more distance per unit of time: faster.

Not quite. Steeper isn't longer. It means more distance each minute: faster.
