Topic 8.8 · Area and Circumference of Circles · Lesson 3 of 4

Area of a Circle

A pizza's price rarely matches its size fairly - a 12 inch pizza has more than twice the area of an 8 inch one, not just 50% more, because area grows with the square of the radius, not the radius itself. Today you learn the circle area formula, then use it in reverse to find a circle's radius from its area.

By the end you’ll know…
  • The area of a circle is A = pi x r².
  • The radius is squared first and then multiplied by pi, since the index applies only to r.
  • Given the diameter, the radius is halved before the formula is used.
  • The radius is found from the area by dividing by pi and then taking the square root.
  • Area is measured in square units and circumference in units of length, which is why the two formulas cannot be swapped.

Squaring before multiplying

Area of a circle: A = π × r². Square the radius first. The index applies to r only, not to π.

The area of a circle is found using A = π × r². The radius is squared first, then multiplied by π, because the index applies only to r, not to π as well.

The radius first, and the units after

Given the diameter, halve it first to get the radius. Area is in square units, such as cm².

If a question gives the diameter rather than the radius, the radius must be found first, by halving, before the formula is used. Area is measured in square units (such as cm²), which is why the area formula cannot be mixed up with the circumference formula, which gives a length.
AREA FROM RADIUS

Undoing the area formula

To find the radius from the area, divide by π. Then square root the result.

To find the radius from a known area, the formula is used in reverse: dividing the area by π and then taking the square root of the result undoes the squaring in the area formula, since squaring and square-rooting are inverse operations.

Exact or rounded

Leave an answer in terms of π when asked for it exactly. Otherwise round to the stated accuracy.

An answer can be left in terms of π where the question asks for it exactly, or rounded to a stated number of decimal places or significant figures where a decimal answer is wanted.
RADIUS FROM AREA
A circle has an area of 78.5 cm². Find its radius, giving your answer to 1 decimal place.
Write the formulaA = π × r²
Substitute 78.5 for A78.5 = π × r²
Divide both sides by π78.5 ÷ π = r²
24.988... = r²
Square root both sides4.998... = r
Round to 1 d.p.5.0 = r
r = 5.0 cm

1
Explain why A = π × r² requires squaring the radius before multiplying by π, not squaring the whole of π × r.
The formula is π × (r²) - the index only applies to r, since there are no brackets grouping π and r together; squaring the whole of π × r would be a different (and wrong) calculation.
