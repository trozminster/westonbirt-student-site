Exit ticket · 8.8.2
Explain why dividing a circumference by 2×π (rather than just π) gives the radius instead of the diameter.
Circumference = π × diameter =2×π × radius, so dividing by π alone gives the diameter, while dividing by 2×π gives the radius directly.

Exit ticket · 8.8.3
Explain why A=π×r2 requires squaring the radius before multiplying by π, not squaring the whole of π×r.
The formula is π×(r2) - the index only applies to r, since there are no brackets grouping π and r together; squaring the whole of π×r would be a different (and wrong) calculation.
