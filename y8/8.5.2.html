Direct Proportion and Best Buys

Two supermarkets sell the same cereal in different sized boxes at different prices - and "buy one get one free" isn't always the deal it looks like. Today you learn the unitary method to solve any direct proportion problem, then use it to spot the genuine best buy and convert between currencies.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Two quantities are in direct proportion when multiplying one multiplies the other by the same factor.
  • Their ratio therefore stays constant, so doubling one doubles the other.
  • The unitary method finds the value of one unit first, then scales up to the amount wanted.
  • A unit cost makes different pack sizes comparable, so the best buy is the lowest cost per unit.
  • An exchange rate is a direct proportion, so it works the same way in both directions.

Direct proportion

In direct proportion, doubling one quantity doubles the other. Their ratio stays constant. Here 4 pens cost £3

Two quantities are in direct proportion when multiplying one multiplies the other by the same factor - their ratio stays constant, so doubling one doubles the other, and halving one halves the other.

The unitary method

Unitary method: first find the value of ONE unit. Then multiply up to the amount actually wanted.

To solve a direct proportion problem, first find the value of ONE unit (divide by the quantity given), then scale that unit value up to the amount actually wanted (multiply by the new quantity). This works because the two quantities stay in the same constant ratio.
THE UNITARY METHOD
9 identical notebooks cost £13.50
Find the cost of 15 notebooks using the unitary method.
Write what we know9 notebooks=£13.50
Divide both sides by 91 notebook=£1.50(the value of one unit)
Multiply by 1515 notebooks=£22.50(scale up to the amount wanted)
Check£22.50÷15=£1.50

Learn

Best buys

Compare packs by unit cost: the price of one unit. The best buy has the lowest unit cost, not the lowest price.

A unit cost (price per single unit, e.g. price per 100 g) makes different pack sizes directly comparable - the best buy is whichever has the LOWEST cost per unit, not whichever has the lowest total price or the largest pack.

Currency conversion

An exchange rate is a direct proportion. Multiply by the rate to convert one way. Divide by the rate to convert back.

An exchange rate is itself a direct proportion, so it works the same way in both directions - multiply to convert INTO the currency the rate is given in terms of, divide to convert back out of it.
BEST BUY
A 400 g jar of coffee costs £4.80, and a 600 g jar costs £6.90
Which is the better buy?
Divide by 400£4.80÷400=£0.012 per gram(1.2p per gram)
Divide by 600£6.90÷600=£0.0115 per gram(1.15p per gram)
The 600 g jar costs less per gram, even though its total price is higher
Better buythe 600 g jar
YOUR TURN · c
Decide which is the better buy: a 200 g bag of rice at £1.60, or a 500 g bag at £3.50
Hint. Follow the worked example: Divide by 400 → Divide by 600 → Better buy
£1.60÷200=0.8p per gram against £3.50÷500=0.7p per gram, so the 500 g bag is the better buy
YOUR TURN · d
A 250 ml bottle of shampoo costs £2.00, and a 400 ml bottle costs £3.60
Which is the better buy?
Hint. find the cost per ml for each - don't compare the total prices directly.
£2.00÷250=0.8p per ml against £3.60÷400=0.9p per ml, so the 250 ml bottle is the better buy - the bigger bottle is not always cheaper
