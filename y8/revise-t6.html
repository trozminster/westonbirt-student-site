Exit ticket · 8.6.2
Explain why every term in an equation must be multiplied by the denominator (or LCM) when clearing a fraction, not just the fractional term.
Multiplying only the fractional term would unbalance the equation - both sides must be multiplied by the same amount to keep it true, so every term on both sides needs multiplying, not just the one with the fraction.
