Exit ticket · 8.4.1
Explain what it means for two shapes to be similar, and state what the scale factor tells you about the relationship between them.
Similar shapes have equal corresponding angles and sides in the same ratio (same shape, different size). The scale factor tells you what one shape has been multiplied by to get the other.

Exit ticket · 8.4.2
Explain why the two triangles formed when two straight lines cross between a pair of parallel lines are always similar, and name the two arrangements these triangles can form.
Alternate angles are equal and the angles at the crossing are equal, so all three angles match. Arrangements: hourglass and arrowhead.

Exit ticket · 8.4.3
Explain the difference between similar shapes and congruent shapes, and explain why AAA (three matching angles) is not a valid condition for proving triangles congruent.
Similar shapes have the same angles; congruent shapes are also the same size. AAA fails because a scaled copy has the same angles.
