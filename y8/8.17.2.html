Averages from Frequency Tables

When a hundred people answer the same survey, nobody lists a hundred numbers: they count them into a table. Today we find the averages straight from that table.

By the end you’ll know…
  • In a frequency table each value occurs as many times as its frequency states.
  • The mean is therefore the total of value x frequency, divided by the total frequency.
  • The mode is the value with the highest frequency, not the frequency itself.
  • The median is found by counting through the frequencies to the middle position, at (n+1)÷2
  • A missing frequency is found by writing the mean formula as an equation and solving it.

A table is a list in short

Each value occurs as many times as its frequency says. So 1 goal with frequency 8 means eight matches with 1 goal. Their total is value × frequency: 8 goals.

Each value occurs as many times as its frequency says, so 1 goal with frequency 8 means eight matches with 1 goal. Their total is value × frequency, 8 goals.

The mean from a table

The mean is the total of value × frequency divided by the total frequency. Dividing by the number of rows would ignore how often each value occurs.

Mode and median from a table

The mode is the value with the highest frequency, never the frequency itself. The median is the n+12th value, found by counting down the frequencies.

Learn: The mean as an equation

The mean as an equation

Frequency x adds x to the count and x times its value to the total. Set the mean formula equal to the given mean. That gives an equation to solve.

A missing frequency x adds x to the total frequency and x times its value to the total. Setting the mean formula equal to the given mean gives an equation to solve.
WORKED EXAMPLE C
Find x, when values 1, 2, 3 have frequencies 5, x, 2 and mean 1.8
Form the equation(11+2x)÷(7+x)=1.8
Clear the fraction11+2x=1.8(7+x)(multiply both sides by 7+x)
Expand the bracket11+2x=12.6+1.8x
Subtract 1.8x11+0.2x=12.6
Subtract 110.2x=1.6
Divide by 0.2x=8
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Find x, when 0, 1 and 2 goals came in 4, 7 and x matches, with mean 1.25

Clear the fraction7+2x=1.25(11+x)(multiply both sides by 11+x)

Subtract 70.75x=6.75
Divide by 0.75x=9
