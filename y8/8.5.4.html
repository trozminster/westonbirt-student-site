Inverse Proportion

It takes 4 painters 6 hours to paint a fence - but 8 painters could do the same fence faster, not slower or the same. Today you learn inverse proportion: when more of one thing genuinely means less of another, and how to tell it apart from direct proportion before you solve.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Two quantities are in inverse proportion when multiplying one divides the other by the same factor.
  • Their product therefore stays constant, so one known pair of values gives that constant for every other pair.
  • Doubling one halves the other, which is the opposite of direct proportion.
  • More of one meaning less of the other, such as workers and time taken, signals inverse proportion; more meaning more signals direct.

Inverse proportion

In inverse proportion, doubling one quantity halves the other. Their PRODUCT stays constant, not their ratio.

Two quantities are in inverse proportion when multiplying one DIVIDES the other by the same factor - their PRODUCT stays constant, rather than their ratio. Doubling one halves the other; this is the opposite of direct proportion, where doubling one doubles the other.

Solving

One known pair gives the constant product. Divide it by any new value to find its partner.

One known pair of values gives the constant product for every other pair - once you have that constant, divide it by any new value of one quantity to find the matching value of the other.
THE CONSTANT PRODUCT
6 workers can complete a job in 8 hours. How long would it take 4 workers, working at the same rate?
Find the constant product6×8=48(worker-hours needed for the job)
Divide by 4 workers48÷4=12 hours
Checkfewer workers should take LONGER, and 12 hours is longer than 8- consistent with inverse proportion.

the constant product is 5×12=60 machine-hours, so 60÷3=20 hours

Spotting the type

More of one means MORE of the other: direct proportion. More of one means LESS of the other: inverse proportion.

If more of one quantity means MORE of the other, the context is direct proportion. If more of one quantity means LESS of the other, the context is inverse proportion - common inverse contexts include workers and time, speed and time (for a fixed distance), and people sharing a fixed amount of work.

Solving once decided

Decide the type BEFORE choosing a method. Direct uses the unitary method. Inverse uses the constant product.

Direct proportion is solved with the unitary method (find one unit, scale up in the SAME direction). Inverse proportion is solved with the constant product method (multiply the given pair, then divide by the new value) - deciding which type BEFORE choosing a method is the step that matters most.
DIRECT OR INVERSE?
Decide whether each context is direct or inverse proportion, then solve: (a) 5 identical books cost £22.50 - find the cost of 8 books. (b) 5 workers paint a fence in 12 hours - find the time for 8 workers.
(a) More books cost more, so direct proportion
Divide for one book£22.50÷5=£4.50
Multiply by 8£4.50×8=£36
(b) More workers take less time, so inverse proportion
Find the constant product5×12=60
Divide by 8 workers60÷8=7.5 hours
YOUR TURN · c
Direct or inverse: if 6 identical tickets cost £51, find the cost of 10 tickets.
Hint. Follow the worked example: Divide for one book → Multiply by 8 → Find the constant product → Divide by 8 workers
direct - more tickets means more cost. £51÷6=£8.50 each, so 10×£8.50=£85
YOUR TURN · d
Direct or inverse? 6 pumps drain a pool in 9 hours - find the time for 4 pumps.
Hint. does more of the first quantity mean more, or less, of the second?
inverse - more pumps means less time. The constant product is 6×9=54, so 54÷4=13.5 hours.
