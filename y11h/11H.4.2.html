Conditional Probability from Venn Diagrams

Surveys sort people into overlapping groups, and many questions ask about one group among the members of another. A Venn diagram makes the restriction visible: the condition is a circle, and nothing outside it counts.

By the end you’ll know…
  • On a Venn diagram, P(A | B) uses only the elements inside circle B, so P(A | B)=n(A and B)÷n(B).
  • The numerator is the overlap and the denominator is the whole of B: B only plus the overlap.
  • P(A | B) and P(B | A) share a numerator but have different denominators, so they are usually different.
  • When the Venn diagram holds probabilities instead of counts, the same rule applies: P(A | B)=P(A and B)÷P(B).

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Only the given circle counts

Given B, only circle B is possible. P (A | B) is the overlap over the whole of B.

Given B, only the elements inside circle B are possible, both the B only region and the overlap. So P (A | B) is the overlap divided by the whole of B.

Swap the condition, swap the denominator

Both use the overlap on top. One divides by n(B), the other by n(A).

P (A | B) and P (B | A) share the overlap as their numerator. Their denominators are the two different circles, so the answers usually differ.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

A given probability fixes a count

A conditional probability fixes a count. Set overlap over circle equal to it, with the unknown as x.

A conditional probability is a count over a restricted total, so knowing it fixes one of those counts. Writing the unknown region as x, the overlap over the circle is set equal to the probability given.

Solve, then use the region

Clear the fraction and solve for the count. The completed diagram then answers the rest.

Clearing the fraction gives a linear equation for the unknown count. The completed diagram then answers any further question.
WORKED EXAMPLE C
A Venn diagram has 9 in A only, x in both and 6 in B only. P (A | B)=25
Find x.
Write the conditionxx+6=25
Multiply both sides by 5(x+6)5x=2(x+6)
=2x+12
Subtract 2x from both sides3x=12
x=4
