Topic 11H.2 · Further Trigonometry · Lesson 6 of 6

Combining the Trigonometry Rules

Real problems, such as a ship's course or a plot of land, rarely give one ready-made triangle. Today we split shapes into triangles and chain the sine rule, cosine rule and area formula together.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A multi-step problem is split into triangles, each solved with the rule its given information fits.
  • A side-and-opposite-angle pair calls for the sine rule; two sides and the angle between them, or three sides, call for the cosine rule; and two sides with the angle between them give the area by 12 ab sinC.
  • A length or angle found in one triangle becomes given information in the next, kept to full calculator accuracy and rounded only at the end.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

One triangle feeds the next

Split the shape into triangles. A length found in one becomes given information in the next.

Keep full accuracy

Store each length in the calculator and round only at the end. Rounding early changes the final answer.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
In quadrilateral ABCD, AB=7 cm, BC=10 cm and angle ABC=65°. Angle CAD=40° and angle ADC=75°. Find CD.
Cosine rule in ABCAC²=49+100−140cos65°
Store the lengthAC=9.478...
Sine rule in ACDCD=AC sin40°÷sin75°
CD=6.31 cm
WORKED EXAMPLE B
In quadrilateral PQRS, PQ=8 cm, QR=6 cm and angle PQR =110°.PS =9 cm and angle RPS=35°. Find the area of PQRS.
Area of PQR12×8×6×sin110°≈22.55
Cosine rule for PRPR²=100−96cos110°
PR=11.53
Area of PRS12×PR×9×sin35°≈29.75
area=52.3 cm²

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Turn bearings into angles

A journey of two legs makes a triangle. The angle at the turn comes from the back bearing.

Back to a bearing

An angle inside the triangle turns back into a bearing. Add it to the bearing it was measured from.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
A ship sails 8 km from A to B on a bearing of 060°, then 5 km from B to C on a bearing of 160°. Find AC.
Back bearing060°+180°=240°
Angle at B240°−160°=80°
Two sides and the angle betweenAC²=64+25−80cos80°
AC=8.67 km
WORKED EXAMPLE D
For the same journey, find the bearing of C from A.
A pair is knownBC and angle ABC
Sine rulesin BAC=5sin80°÷8.667
Angle BAC34.6°
bearing=060°+34.6°=094.6°
