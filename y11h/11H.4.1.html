Conditional Probability from Tables

Medical tests, insurance and forecasts all quote probabilities 'given' something already known. Knowing one fact shrinks the set of possible outcomes, and a table or grid shows exactly which outcomes are left.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A sample space lists every equally likely outcome, in a two-way table or a grid such as the 36 outcomes of two dice.
  • A probability is the count of wanted outcomes over the count of all possible outcomes.
  • A conditional probability is a probability GIVEN that something is already known, and the known fact shrinks the possible outcomes to one row, column or part of the grid.
  • So P(A given B) = (number in both A and B) ÷ (number in B): the denominator is the restricted total, not the grand total.

Given shrinks what is possible

A probability is the wanted count over everything possible. 'Given' shrinks what is possible to one row or column.

A probability is the count of wanted outcomes over the count of everything possible. The word 'given' shrinks what is possible to one row or column of a table, so that row's or column's total becomes the denominator.

The condition decides the denominator

The condition is the denominator. The same cell over a different total gives a different probability.

P(tennis given boy) divides the 14 boys who play tennis by all 30 boys. P(boy given tennis) divides the same 14 by all 23 tennis players, so the two answers differ.

Every outcome, equally likely

A grid lists every equally likely outcome: two dice give 36 cells. Count cells, never the different totals.

A sample space grid lists every equally likely outcome of two events, so two dice give 6×6=36 cells. A probability counts cells; the eleven totals are not equally likely, because each total fills a different number of cells.

Keep only what can still happen

'Given the first die shows 4' leaves 6 cells. Count the wanted cells there and divide by 6

'Given the first die shows 4' leaves only the 6 cells in that row. The wanted cells are counted inside that row and divided by 6, not by 36
