Midpoints and Ratios on Lines

A point halfway along a line, or a third of the way, is still reached by a route of known vectors. Today we find vectors to midpoints and to points that split a line in a ratio.

By the end you’ll know…
  • If M is the midpoint of AB, then vector AM = 12 vector AB.
  • If P divides AB in the ratio m:n, then vector AP = mm+n of vector AB, because the line is split into m+n equal parts.
  • The vector to such a point from O is found by a route: vector OP = vector OA + vector AP.
  • With vector OA = a and vector OB = b, vector AB = b−a, so vector OM = a+12(b−a)=12(a+b).
By the end you can
G8Find the vector to the midpoint of a line, such as vector OM, in terms of a and b.
G8Find the vector to a point dividing a line in a given ratio, such as AP:PB = 1:3, in terms of a and b.

02

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Halfway along

The midpoint M is halfway along AB, in the same direction. So AM→ is half of AB→.

Start the route at O

To reach M from O, go to A first, then halfway along AB. So OM→ = a+12(b−a)=12(a+b).

WORKED EXAMPLE A
OA→ = a, OB→ = b and M is the midpoint of AB. Find OM→.
Find AB→ firstAB→=b−a
Go to A, then halfwayOM→=a+12(b−a)
OM→=12(a+b)
WORKED EXAMPLE B
OABC is a parallelogram with OA→ = a and OC→ = c. M is the midpoint of AB. Find OM→.
Use the opposite sideAB→=c
Go to A, then halfwayOM→=a+12c
YOUR TURN · a
OP→ = p, OQ→ = q and N is the midpoint of PQ. Find PN→ and ON→.
PN→ = 12(q−p); ON→ = 12(p+q)
YOUR TURN · b
ABCD is a parallelogram with AB→ = x and AD→ = y. M is the midpoint of DC. Find AM→.
AM→ = y+12x

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

A ratio cuts equal parts

A ratio m : n cuts a line into m+n equal parts. So AP : PB = 1 : 3 puts P a quarter along.

The same fraction of the vector

The same fraction applies to the vector. So AP→ = ¹⁄₄AB→, and OP→ = a+14(b−a).

WORKED EXAMPLE C
OA→ = a and OB→ = b. P is on AB with AP : PB = 2 : 3
Find OP→.
Turn the ratio into a fractionAP→=25AB→
Go to A, then alongOP→=a+25(b−a)
OP→=35a+25b
WORKED EXAMPLE D
OA→ = a and OB→ = b. Q is on OB with OQ : QB = 3 : 1
Find AQ→.
Find OQ→OQ→=34b
Go A to O to QAQ→=−a+34b
YOUR TURN · c
OA→ = a and OB→ = b. P is on AB with AP : PB = 1 : 2
Find OP→.
OP→ = a+13(b−a)=23a+13b
YOUR TURN · d
R is on AB with AR : RB = 3 : 1
Find OR→ and BR→.
OR→ = a+34(b−a)=14a+34b; BR→ = 14(a−b)
