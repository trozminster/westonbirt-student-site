Stationary Points and Increasing Functions

The highest point of a ball's flight and the lowest cost of a product are both places where a curve turns. There the gradient is exactly zero, and that single fact finds them.

By the end you’ll know…
  • At a stationary point the tangent is horizontal, so the gradient is zero: stationary points are found by solving dydx = 0.
  • Solving gives the x-coordinate; substituting it into the equation of the curve gives the y-coordinate.
  • A maximum has a positive gradient just before it and a negative gradient just after; a minimum is the other way round.
  • A function is increasing where dydx > 0 and decreasing where dydx < 0.
By the end you can
G8Find the coordinates of the stationary points of a quadratic or cubic curve by solving dydx = 0.
G9Decide whether a stationary point is a maximum or a minimum from the gradient either side, and find where a function is increasing or decreasing.

The gradient is zero at a turning point

At a turning point the tangent is flat, so the gradient is zero. Solving dydx = 0 finds it.

At the top or bottom of a curve the tangent is horizontal, so its gradient is zero. Stationary points are found by solving dydx = 0.

Both coordinates are needed

Solving dydx = 0 gives only x. Substituting into y gives the other coordinate.

Solving dydx = 0 gives only the x-coordinate. Substituting it into the equation of the curve gives the y-coordinate.

Learn

The gradient's sign either side

Before a maximum the gradient is positive, and after it negative. A minimum goes negative, then positive.

Just before a maximum the gradient is positive, and just after it is negative. A minimum goes from negative to positive, so the signs either side classify the point.

Increasing means a positive gradient

Increasing means dydx is positive. On y = x² − 6x, that is where x > 3.

A function is increasing where dydx is positive and decreasing where dydx is negative. On y = x² - 6x, dydx = 2x - 6 is positive for x > 3.
WORKED EXAMPLE C
y = x³ - 12x + 1 has stationary points at x = 2 and x = -2. Decide which is a maximum.
Gradient at x = -3: 3 × 9 - 12 = 15
Gradient at x = -1: 3 × 1 - 12 = -9
Conclusion+ then -, so x = -2 is a maximum So x = 2 is the minimum: -9 at x = 1
then +15 at x = 3.
