Topic 11H.7 · Differentiation · Lesson 2 of 6

Differentiating Powers of x

Drawing tangents by eye only ever gives an estimate. Differentiation gives the exact gradient of a curve at every point from its equation, with no drawing at all.

By the end you’ll know…
  • The gradient function dydx gives the gradient of a curve at every point.
  • For y = xⁿ, dydx = n xⁿ⁻¹: the power multiplies, then drops by one; so y = a xⁿ gives dydx = a n xⁿ⁻¹.
  • A term in x differentiates to its coefficient, and a constant differentiates to 0, because its graph is flat.
  • A sum or difference is differentiated term by term.
  • The gradient at a point is found by substituting its x-value into dydx, not into y.
By the end you can
G8Differentiate expressions of the form a xⁿ for positive integer n, term by term, including x terms and constants.
G8Find the gradient of a curve at a given point by differentiating and substituting the x-value.

The power comes down and drops by one

The gradient function dydx gives the gradient at every point. For y = xⁿ, the power comes down and drops by one.

The gradient function dydx gives the gradient of the curve at every point. For y = xⁿ, dydx = nxⁿ⁻¹: the power multiplies and then drops by one.

Term by term

A sum is differentiated term by term. A term in x gives its coefficient, and a constant gives 0.

A sum is differentiated one term at a time. A term in x differentiates to its coefficient, and a constant to 0, because its graph is flat.

Substitute into the gradient function

The gradient at a point puts its x into dydx, not into y. On y = x², the gradient at x = 3 is 6.

The gradient at one point comes from putting its x-value into dydx, not into y. On y = x², dydx = 2x, so the gradient at x = 3 is 6.

The sign shows the direction

A negative gradient means the curve falls there. Zero means it is momentarily flat.

A negative value of dydx means the curve is falling there, and zero means it is momentarily flat. On y = x², the gradient is -4 at x = -2 and 0 at x = 0.
