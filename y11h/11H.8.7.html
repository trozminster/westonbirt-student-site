Topic 11H.8 · Vectors · Lesson 7 of 8

Proving Lines Parallel

Vectors can prove that two lines are parallel without measuring an angle. Today we write proofs that end with a scalar multiple and a sentence.

By the end you’ll know…
  • To prove two lines are parallel, both vectors are written in terms of a and b, and one is shown to be a scalar multiple of the other.
  • A common factor may need taking out first: 2a+4b =2(a+2b), so it is parallel to a+2b.
  • The proof states the multiple and concludes in words: vector PQ=2 x vector RS, so PQ is parallel to RS and twice as long.
  • A quadrilateral is a parallelogram when both pairs of opposite sides are equal vectors, and a trapezium when one pair is parallel but not equal.

Parallel means a multiple

Two lines are parallel when one vector is a scalar multiple of the other. A common factor may need taking out first.

Say what it shows

A proof ends by stating the multiple in words. The midpoints of two sides of a triangle give the classic example.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
OA→ = a and OB→ = b. P and Q are the midpoints of OA and OB. Prove that PQ is parallel to AB.
Find both vectorsPQ→=12(b−a)
AB→=b−a
Compare themPQ→=12AB→
so PQ is parallel to AB, and half as long
WORKED EXAMPLE B
PQ→ = 3a−6b and RS→ = −a+2b. Prove that PQ is parallel to RS.
Take out a factorPQ→=−3(−a+2b)
Parallel, three times as longPQ→=−3RS→

Parallelogram or trapezium

A parallelogram has opposite sides that are equal vectors. A trapezium has one pair parallel but not equal.

Every side first

Every side is first written in terms of a and b. Comparing the opposite pairs then decides the shape.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
OA→ = 2a, OC→ = c and CB→ = a. Prove that OABC is a trapezium.
Compare OA→ and CB→OA→=2CB→, so OA ∥ CB
Check the other pairAB→=−2a+c+a
=c - a, not a multiple of c
one pair parallel only, so a trapezium
WORKED EXAMPLE D
PQ→ = 2a+b, SR→ = 2a+b and PS→ = a−b. Prove that PQRS is a parallelogram.
Compare PQ→ and SR→they are equal
Find QR→−(2a+b) +(a−b) +(2a+b) =a−b
= PS→
both pairs equal, so a parallelogram
YOUR TURN · c
OPQR has OP→ = 3a, OR→ = b and RQ→ = a. Prove that it is a trapezium.
Hint. Follow the worked example: Compare OA→ and CB→ → Check the other pair
OP→ = 3RQ→, so OP ∥ RQ; PQ→ = b−2a is not parallel to b, so a trapezium
YOUR TURN · d
ABCD has AB→ = 2p - q and DC→ = 2p - q. Explain why ABCD is a parallelogram.
Hint. Follow the worked example: Compare PQ→ and SR→ → Find QR→
AB→ = DC→: one pair of opposite sides equal and parallel, so AD→ = BC→ too
