Topic 11H.9 · Functions and Graph Transformations · Lesson 10 of 11

Horizontal Graph Transformations

A change inside the bracket acts on the input before the rule does, so the graph moves the opposite way to the sign. Today: horizontal translations, stretches and reflections, and how to tell all six transformations apart.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A change inside the bracket acts on the inputs, so it moves the graph horizontally and every y-coordinate stays the same.
  • y = f(x + a) translates the graph left by a, the opposite way to the sign, so (p, q) maps to (p - a, q) and y = f(x - 3) is the graph moved right 3.
  • y = f(ax) stretches the graph horizontally by scale factor 1a, so (p, q) maps to (pa, q) and points on the y-axis stay put.
  • y = f(-x) reflects the graph in the y-axis, so (p, q) maps to (-p, q).
By the end you can
G8Sketch y = f(x + a) and y = f(-x) from the graph of y = f(x), and give the image of a point.
G9Sketch y = f(ax) from the graph of y = f(x), and decide which transformation maps one given graph onto another.

Inside acts on inputs

In y = f(x + 2), every output is reached 2 earlier. So the graph moves left 2, against the sign.

A minus inside flips sideways

In y = f(-x), every input changes sign. So the graph is reflected in the y-axis.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Multiplying inside squashes

In y = f(2x), every output is reached at half the input. So the graph is stretched horizontally, scale factor 12.

Six transformations, two places

Outside the bracket acts on y; inside acts on x, the opposite way. Adding translates, multiplying stretches, and a minus reflects.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
(6, 2) lies on y = f(x). Give its image on y = f(3x) and on y = f(0.5x).

Divide x by 0.5(12, 2)
WORKED EXAMPLE D
y = f(x) has roots -4 and 6. y = g(x) has roots -2 and 3, and the same least value. Which transformation maps f to g?
Compare the rootsevery x is halved
g(x) = f(2x), a horizontal stretch, scale factor 12

(2, -3) and (16, -3)

(-x)² + 4(-x) = x² - 4x, so it is y = f(-x): a reflection in the y-axis
