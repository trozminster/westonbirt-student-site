Drawing and Measuring Tangents

A speedometer shows speed at one instant, not over a whole journey. On a curved graph that instant is a single point, and today a straight line drawn at that point measures it; next lesson, algebra will do it exactly.

By the end you’ll know…
  • The gradient of a curve changes from point to point, so a rate of change on a curve belongs to one instant.
  • A tangent touches the curve at one point and has the same gradient as the curve there.
  • The tangent's gradient is its rise divided by its run, read from the axis scales, and a large triangle gives a more accurate reading.
  • A chord joins two points on the curve, and its gradient is the average rate of change between them, not the rate at a point.
By the end you can

Learn

The rate changes along a curve

A curve's gradient changes from point to point. A tangent touches the curve and shares its gradient there.

On a curve the gradient is different at every point, so a rate of change belongs to one instant. A tangent touches the curve at that point and shares its gradient there.

Rise over run on the tangent

The gradient is the tangent's rise over its run, on the axis scales. A large triangle reads it more accurately.

The tangent's gradient is its rise divided by its run, read from the axis scales. A large triangle drawn on the tangent makes the reading more accurate.
WORKED EXAMPLE A
The curve shows the volume of water in a tank. Estimate the rate of flow at 3 minutes.
Modeldraw the line touching the curve at t=3 only, then a triangle on it 2 across
12 up: 12÷2=6 litres per minute.
WORKED EXAMPLE B
The curve shows the temperature of a cooling drink. Estimate its rate of change at 4 minutes.
Modelthe tangent falls 16 over a run of 2, so the gradient is −8: the drink cools at 8 degrees per minute.

Learn

A chord gives an average rate

A chord joins two points, giving the average rate between them. On y=x2, x=1 to 3 gives 4

A chord joins two points on the curve, so its gradient is the average rate of change between them. On y=x2 the chord from x=1 to x=3 has gradient 4

Closer points, closer gradients

Moving the second point closer brings the gradient towards the tangent's. At x=2 on y=x2, that is 4

As the second point moves closer, the chord's gradient gets closer to the tangent's. At x=2 on y=x2, the chords close in on 4
