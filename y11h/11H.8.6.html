Ratios in Vector Problems

Exam vector questions often place points beyond the end of a line, or on two different sides of a shape. Today we combine those ratios into routes.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A point on a line extended beyond B, such as C with AB:BC = 2:1, is reached by carrying on in the same direction, so vector BC = 12 vector AB.
  • A point on a side of a shape is reached by a route along known vectors, using the fraction of that side.
  • Every vector in a problem is written in terms of the same two non-parallel vectors, such as a and b, and simplified by collecting terms.
By the end you can
G8Find the vector to a point on a line extended beyond a vertex, given a ratio.
G8Find vectors in a shape that combine points given by ratios on two different sides.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Carry on past B

A point beyond B continues in the same direction as AB. With AB : BC = 2 : 1, BC→ is half of AB→.

Parts, then the whole

The whole line AC is 3 parts, so AC→ = ³⁄₂AB→. A ratio compares parts, not a part with the whole.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
OA→ = a and OB→ = b. C is on AB extended with AB : BC = 3 : 1
Find OC→.
Carry on past BBC→=13(b−a)
Go O to B to COC→=b+13(b−a)
OC→=43b−13a
WORKED EXAMPLE B
OA→ = a. D is on AO extended so that OD = 2 × OA. Find AD→.
Go back along aAO→=−a
Carry on twice as farOD→=−2a
AD→=−3a
YOUR TURN · a
OA→ = a and OB→ = b. C is on AB extended with AB : BC = 1 : 1
Find OC→.
OC→ = b+(b−a)=2b−a
YOUR TURN · b
OP→ = p and OQ→ = q. R is on PQ extended with PQ : QR = 2 : 1
Find PR→.
PR→ = ³⁄₂(q - p)

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Points on two sides

Each point gets its own route from a vertex. The vector between two such points is then a route through a vertex.

Collect at the end

A route through A gives PQ→ = PA→ + AQ→, two known pieces. The a terms and the b terms are collected last.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
OA→ = a and OB→ = b. P is on OA with OP : PA = 1 : 2, and Q is on AB with AQ : QB = 1 : 2
Find PQ→.
Find the piecesPA→=23a
AQ→=13(b−a)
Go P to A to QPQ→=23a+13b−13a
PQ→=13a+13b
WORKED EXAMPLE D
OABC is a parallelogram with OA→ = a and OC→ = c. M is the midpoint of AB, and N is on CB with CN : NB = 1 : 3
Find MN→.
Find the piecesMB→=12c
BN→=−34a
MN→=12c−34a
YOUR TURN · c
OA→ = a and OB→ = b. P is the midpoint of OA and Q is the midpoint of OB. Find PQ→.
PQ→ = -¹⁄₂a + ¹⁄₂b
YOUR TURN · d
ABCD is a parallelogram with AB→ = x and AD→ = y. M is the midpoint of BC and N is the midpoint of CD. Find MN→.
MC→ = ¹⁄₂y, CN→ = −12x, so MN→ = ¹⁄₂y - 12x
