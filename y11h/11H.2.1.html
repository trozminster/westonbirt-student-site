Graphs of Sin, Cos and Tan

Sine, cosine and tangent work for any angle, not just those in a right-angled triangle. Today we sketch their graphs and use them to find every solution of an equation.

By the end you’ll know…
  • y=sinx and y=cosx are waves that repeat every 360°, with values from −1 to 1
  • y=sinx starts at 0, rises to 1 at 90°, is 0 at 180°,−1 at 270° and 0 again at 360°; y=cosx has the same shape, starting at 1
  • y=tanx repeats every 180°, passes through 0 at 0 and 180°, and is undefined at 90 and 270°, where the graph has asymptotes.
  • By the symmetry of the graphs, an equation such as sinx=0.5 has two solutions from 0 to 360°: 30° and 180−30=150°.

Sine and cosine are waves

Both graphs repeat every 360°, between −1 and 1
Sine starts at 0, but cosine starts at 1

Tangent has gaps

The tangent graph repeats every 180°. At 90° and 270° it has no value, so the graph has asymptotes there.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Sine is symmetrical about 90°

The calculator gives one solution. The graph's symmetry gives the other: 180° minus the first.

Cosine is symmetrical about 180°

For cosine, the second solution is 360° minus the first.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
Solve sinx=0.6 for 0°≤x≤360°.

Symmetry of sine180°−36.9°=143.1°
x=36.9° or 143.1°
