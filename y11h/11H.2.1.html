Graphs of Sin, Cos and Tan

Sine, cosine and tangent work for any angle, not just those in a right-angled triangle. Today we sketch their graphs and use them to find every solution of an equation.

By the end you’ll know…
  • y = sin x and y = cos x are waves that repeat every 360 degrees, with values from -1 to 1.
  • y = sin x starts at 0, rises to 1 at 90 degrees, is 0 at 180 degrees, -1 at 270 degrees and 0 again at 360 degrees; y = cos x has the same shape, starting at 1.
  • y = tan x repeats every 180 degrees, passes through 0 at 0 and 180 degrees, and is undefined at 90 and 270 degrees, where the graph has asymptotes.
  • By the symmetry of the graphs, an equation such as sin x = 0.5 has two solutions from 0 to 360 degrees: 30 degrees and 180 - 30 = 150 degrees.

02

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Sine and cosine are waves

Both graphs repeat every 360°, between -1 and 1. Sine starts at 0, but cosine starts at 1.

Tangent has gaps

The tangent graph repeats every 180°. At 90° and 270° it has no value, so the graph has asymptotes there.

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Sine is symmetrical about 90°

The calculator gives one solution. The graph's symmetry gives the other: 180° minus the first.

Cosine is symmetrical about 180°

For cosine, the second solution is 360° minus the first.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
Solve sin x = 0.6 for 0° ≤ x ≤ 360°.
Calculator valuex = 36.9°
Symmetry of sine180° - 36.9° = 143.1°
x = 36.9° or 143.1°
