Proving Points Collinear

Three points lie on one straight line exactly when their vectors say so. Today we prove points are collinear, and find unknowns that make them so.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Three points A, B and C are collinear when vector AB is a scalar multiple of vector BC, or of vector AC, and the two vectors share the point B.
  • Parallel alone is not enough: parallel vectors with no point in common lie on separate parallel lines.
  • The scalar gives the ratio: vector AC=3 x vector AB means AB:BC = 1:2
  • An unknown scalar k is found by making one vector a multiple of the other and comparing the coefficients of a and b, since a and b are not parallel.
By the end you can
G9Prove that three points are collinear using vectors, and state the ratio in which the middle point divides the line.
G9Find the value of an unknown scalar, such as k, that makes three points collinear or two lines parallel, by comparing coefficients.

Parallel and sharing a point

Points on one line give parallel vectors that share a point. Parallel alone could mean two separate lines.

The multiple gives the ratio

If AC→ = 3AB→, then AC is three parts and AB is one. So AB : BC=1 : 2

WORKED EXAMPLE A
OA→ = a, OB→ = b and OC→ = 3b−2a. Prove that A, B and C are collinear.
Find two vectorsAB→=b−a
BC→=2b−2a
Compare themBC→=2AB→
parallel and sharing B, so collinear
WORKED EXAMPLE B
OP→ = p, OQ→ = q and OR→ = 4q−3p. Prove that P, Q and R are collinear, and find PQ : QR.
Find two vectorsPQ→=q−p
QR→=3q−3p
Compare, sharing QQR→=3PQ→
Collinear, so the ratioPQ : QR=1 : 3

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Match a with a, b with b

a and b are not parallel, so a vector equation in them splits in two. The a terms match, and the b terms match.

An unknown on a straight line

Collinear points need one vector to be a multiple of the other. The multiple gets a letter, and the coefficients are compared.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
3a+kb is parallel to 2a−5b. Find k.
Write a multiple3a+kb=m(2a−5b)
Match the a termsm=1.5
Match the b termsk=−7.5
