Equations and Algebraic Reasoning

Solving the equation is the easy half - the harder half is a 'show that' question, where you are handed the answer and marked on the route to it, which is the first time this course asks you to write algebra that convinces a reader rather than just lands on a number.

By the end you’ll know…
  • An equation containing fractions is cleared by multiplying every term by the LCM of the denominators.
  • A 'show that' question requires the algebra to arrive at the stated result, not merely verify it numerically.
By the end you can
G6Solve linear equations with brackets on both sides and with fractional terms, and form equations from worded contexts.
G7Express one variable as a percentage or fraction of another, prove a given algebraic identity, and manipulate ratios expressed with variables.

02

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Clear the fractions

Every term is multiplied by the LCM of the denominators. Then the equation has no fractions left.

Show that

A show-that question gives the answer. The marks are for the algebra that reaches it.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
Solve x + 14 - x - 23 = 1.
Multiply every term by 12. Each numerator keeps its bracket.
Multiply by 123(x + 1) - 4(x - 2) = 12
Expand3x + 3 - 4x + 8 = 12
-x + 11 = 12, so x = -1
WORKED EXAMPLE B
Ann is x years old and her father is 3x. In 12 years he will be twice her age. Show that x = 12.
Form the equation from the words, then solve itnever substitute 12.
In 12 yearsAnn x + 12, father 3x + 12
Twice her age3x + 12 = 2(x + 12)
3x + 12 = 2x + 24, so x = 12
YOUR TURN · a
Solve x4 + 2 = x5 + 3.
Multiply by 20: 5x + 40 = 4x + 60, so x = 20.
YOUR TURN · b
A rectangle is 3 cm longer than it is wide, and its perimeter is 46 cm. Show that its width is 10 cm.
2(w + w + 3) = 46, so 4w + 6 = 46 and w = 10 cm.

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

One as a percentage of another

The value of x as a percentage of y is xy × 100. The quantity named first goes on top.

Ratios with letters

If a : b = 2 : 3, then a is 25 of the total. So a = 2k and b = 3k for some number k.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
A class has x boys and y girls. Write the number of boys as a percentage of the class.
The whole class is x + y, so it goes on the bottom.
The classx + y pupils
Boys as a fractionxx + y
Percentage100xx + y%
WORKED EXAMPLE D
a : b = 3 : 4 and a + b = 35. Find a and b.
Write a and b as multiples of k.
a = 3k and b = 4k
7k = 35, so k = 5
a = 15 and b = 20
YOUR TURN · c
Show that the sum of three consecutive integers is always a multiple of 3.
n + (n + 1) + (n + 2) = 3n + 3 = 3(n + 1), a multiple of 3.
YOUR TURN · d
p : q = 5 : 2. Write p as a fraction of p + q.
There are 7 parts, so p is 57 of p + q.
