The Equation of a Circle

A circle is every point at a fixed distance from its centre, so Pythagoras gives its equation, and completing the square turns an expanded equation back into centre and radius.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A circle is the locus of points at a fixed distance from its centre, so Pythagoras gives x2+y2=r2 for a circle centred on the origin and (x−a)2+(y−b)2=r2 for centre (a,b).

Pythagoras gives the equation

Every point on a circle is a distance r from its centre. For centre (a,b) this gives (x−a)2+(y−b)2=r2

Every point (x,y) on the circle is a distance r from the centre. For centre (a,b) this gives (x−a)2+(y−b)2=r2
With the centre at the origin it is x2+y2=r2

Reading the centre and radius

The bracket signs are the opposite of the centre's. The right-hand side is the radius squared.

The signs in the brackets are the opposite of the centre, and the number on the right is the radius squared. So (x−3)2+(y+2)2=49 has centre (3,−2) and radius 7

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Recognising a circle

A circle's x2 and y2 have the same coefficient, with no xy term. Unequal coefficients mean it is not a circle.

In expanded form a circle has x2 and y2 with the same coefficient and no xy term. An equation with unequal coefficients is not a circle.

Completing the square twice

Completing the square in x and y separately reveals the centre. The right-hand side, r2, must be positive.

Complete the square in x and in y separately, and move the numbers to the right-hand side. The right-hand side is r2, and it must be positive.
WORKED EXAMPLE C
Find the centre and radius of x2+y2−8x+2y−8=0
Complete the square in x and in y, then move the constants across.
x2−8x=(x−4)2−16 and y2+2y=(y+1)2−1
(x−4)2+(y+1)2=8+16+1=25
Centre (4,−1) and radius 5
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Find the centre and radius of x2+y2+6x−10y+18=0
The constant 18 moves to the right as −18
x2+6x=(x+3)2−9 and y2−10y=(y−5)2−25
(x+3)2+(y−5)2=−18+9+25=16
Centre (−3,5) and radius 4
YOUR TURN · c
Find the centre and radius of x2+y2−2x−6y−15=0
x2−2x=(x−1)2−1 and y2−6y=(y−3)2−9
So (x−1)2+(y−3)2=15+1+9=25
Centre (1,3), radius 5
YOUR TURN · d
Find the centre and radius of x2+y2+4x+8y+4=0
x2+4x=(x+2)2−4 and y2+8y=(y+4)2−16
So (x+2)2+(y+4)2=−4+4+16=16
Centre (−2,−4), radius 4
