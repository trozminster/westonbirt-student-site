The Second Derivative

Differentiating twice measures how the gradient itself is changing. In motion it turns a formula for position into velocity, and then into acceleration.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Differentiating a second time gives the rate of change of the gradient.
  • In a kinematics context the first derivative is velocity and the second is acceleration.

02

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Differentiate again

Differentiating dydx again gives the second derivative, d²ydx². It measures how fast the gradient itself is changing.

Reading its sign

A positive second derivative means the gradient is increasing. A negative one means it is decreasing.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
Find d²ydx² for y = 2x⁴ - 3x² + 5x.Differentiate dydx, not y, the second time.
First derivativedydx = 8x³ - 6x + 5
Differentiate againd²ydx² = 24x² - 6
WORKED EXAMPLE B
For y = x³ - 6x², find the rate of change of the gradient at x = 1.The rate of change of the gradient is the second derivative.
dydx = 3x² - 12x
d²ydx² = 6x - 12
At x = 1: -6, so the gradient is decreasing
YOUR TURN · a
Find dydx and d²ydx² for y = (x + 2)(x² - 1).
Expand: y = x³ + 2x² - x - 2. dydx = 3x² + 4x - 1 and d²ydx² = 6x + 4.
YOUR TURN · b
For y = 4x, find d²ydx² at x = 2.
y = 4x⁻¹, dydx = -4x⁻² and d²ydx² = 8x⁻³. At x = 2 it is 8 ÷ 8 = 1.

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Position to acceleration

Velocity is the rate of change of displacement: v = dsdt. Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity: a = d²sdt².

Reading the signs

Negative velocity means moving backwards. Negative acceleration means the velocity is decreasing.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
A ball's height is h = 20t - 5t² metres after t seconds. Find its velocity and its acceleration at t = 1.Differentiate once for velocity and twice for acceleration, then substitute t = 1.
Velocityv = dhdt = 20 - 10t = 10 m/s at t = 1
Accelerationa = d²hdt² = -10 m/s²
The ball rises, but its velocity is falling
WORKED EXAMPLE D
For the same ball, when is it highest, and how high is it?At the top the ball stops rising for an instant: v = 0.
Highest when v = 0: 20 - 10t = 0, so t = 2
Heighth = 40 - 20
= 20 m
YOUR TURN · c
A particle moves s = t³ - 6t² + 9t metres after t seconds. Find its velocity and acceleration at t = 2.
v = 3t² - 12t + 9 = -3 m/s and a = 6t - 12 = 0 m/s² at t = 2.
YOUR TURN · d
When is the same particle at rest?
v = 0: 3(t² - 4t + 3) = 0, so t = 1 or t = 3 seconds.
