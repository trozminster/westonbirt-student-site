The Second Derivative

Differentiating twice measures how the gradient itself is changing. In motion it turns a formula for position into velocity, and then into acceleration.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Differentiating a second time gives the rate of change of the gradient.
  • In a kinematics context the first derivative is velocity and the second is acceleration.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Differentiate again

Differentiating dy/dx again gives the second derivative, d2ydx2
It measures how fast the gradient itself is changing.

Reading its sign

A positive second derivative means the gradient is increasing. A negative one means it is decreasing.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
Find d2ydx2 for y=2x4−3x2+5x.
Differentiate dy/dx, not y, the second time.
First derivativedydx=8x3−6x+5
Differentiate againd2ydx2=24x2−6
WORKED EXAMPLE B
For y=x3−6x2, find the rate of change of the gradient at x=1
The rate of change of the gradient is the second derivative.
dy/dx = 3x2−12x
d2ydx2=6x−12
At x=1: −6, so the gradient is decreasing
YOUR TURN · a
Find dy/dx and d2ydx2 for y=(x+2)(x2−1).
Expand: y=x3+2x2−x−2
dy/dx = 3x2+4x−1 and d2ydx2=6x+4
YOUR TURN · b
For y=4x, find d2ydx2 at x=2
y=4x−1, dy/dx = −4x−2 and d2ydx2=8x−3
At x=2 it is 8÷8=1

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Position to acceleration

Velocity is the rate of change of displacement: v = ds/dt. Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity: a=d2sdt2

Reading the signs

Negative velocity means moving backwards. Negative acceleration means the velocity is decreasing.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
A ball's height is h=20t−5t2 metres after t seconds. Find its velocity and its acceleration at t=1
Differentiate once for velocity and twice for acceleration, then substitute t=1
Velocityv=dhdt=20−10t=10m/satt=1
Accelerationa=d2hdt2=−10m/s2
The ball rises, but its velocity is falling
WORKED EXAMPLE D
For the same ball, when is it highest, and how high is it?
At the top the ball stops rising for an instantv=0
Highest when v=0: 20−10t=0, so t=2
Heighth=40−20
