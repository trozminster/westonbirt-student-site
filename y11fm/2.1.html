Grouping, Quadratics and Difference of Squares

Every factorising question in this chapter is the same move in a different disguise - find what the terms share and take it out - and spotting a difference of two squares, or grouping four terms into two pairs that share a bracket, is what turns a page of algebra into one line.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A four-term expression factorises by grouping.
  • A quadratic factorises by splitting the middle term.
  • a² - b² factorises as (a+b)(a-b), including where a and b are themselves expressions.

Learn

Grouping

Four terms factorise in pairs that share one bracket. So ax + ay + bx + by = (a + b)(x + y).

Splitting the middle term

Splitting the middle term needs two numbers with product ac and sum b. Grouping then finishes it.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
Factorise xy + 3x - 2y - 6.
Take -2 out of the second pair so that both pairs give the same bracket.
Pairsx(y + 3) - 2(y + 3)
Common bracket(y + 3)
(x - 2)(y + 3)
WORKED EXAMPLE B
Factorise 3x² - 10x - 8.
ac = -24: find two numbers with product -24 and sum -10.
Numbers-12 and 2
Split3x² - 12x + 2x - 8 = 3x(x - 4) + 2(x - 4)
(3x + 2)(x - 4)
YOUR TURN · a
Factorise ab + 5a + 2b + 10.
a(b + 5) + 2(b + 5) = (a + 2)(b + 5).
YOUR TURN · b
Factorise 2x² + 9x + 4.
ac = 8, so use 8 and 1: 2x² + 8x + x + 4 = 2x(x + 4) + (x + 4) = (2x + 1)(x + 4).

Difference of two squares

A difference of two squares, a² - b², is (a + b)(a - b). A sum of two squares does not factorise.

Bigger squares

Each square can be a term or a whole bracket. So 9x² - y² = (3x + y)(3x - y).

WORKED EXAMPLE C
Factorise fully 3x² - 75.
Take out the common factor 3, then look for squares.
Common factor3(x² - 25)
Difference of squares3(x + 5)(x - 5)
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Factorise (2x + 1)² - (x - 3)².
Each bracket is one of the squares. Keep the second bracket whole.
As a² - b²(2x + 1 + (x - 3))(2x + 1 - (x - 3))
Simplify each(3x - 2)(x + 4)
YOUR TURN · c
Factorise 16a² - 9b².
(4a)² - (3b)² = (4a + 3b)(4a - 3b).
YOUR TURN · d
Factorise x⁴ - 16 fully.
(x² + 4)(x² - 4) = (x² + 4)(x + 2)(x - 2).
