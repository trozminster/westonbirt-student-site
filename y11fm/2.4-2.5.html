Fractional Equations and Completing the Square

Clearing the fractions turns a frightening equation into an ordinary one, and completing the square rewrites any quadratic so its turning point can be read straight off - the form the next two chapters use to sketch a curve and to solve.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Multiplying an equation through by the LCM must be applied to every term, including whole expressions in brackets.
  • A quadratic ax² + bx + c completes the square to a(x + p)² + q; expanding a(x + p)² + q and comparing coefficients with ax² + bx + c pins down p and q, including when a is not 1.
By the end you can
G7Solve linear equations containing algebraic fractions by clearing denominators.
G8Express a quadratic in completed-square form, including with a non-unit leading coefficient, by expanding and comparing coefficients.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Clear every denominator

Every term is multiplied by the LCM of the denominators. A whole number on its own is multiplied too.

Brackets on numerators

A numerator with two terms is bracketed when it is multiplied. So 3 × (x - 1) is 3x - 3.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
Solve x + 23 - x - 14 = 2.
Multiply every term by 12, the right-hand side included.
Multiply by 124(x + 2) - 3(x - 1) = 24
Expand4x + 8 - 3x + 3 = 24
x = 13
WORKED EXAMPLE B
Solve 4x + 1 = 3x - 2.
Multiply both sides by (x + 1)(x - 2).
Multiply4(x - 2) = 3(x + 1)
Expand4x - 8 = 3x + 3
x = 11

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Completing the square

The square completes as x² + bx = (x + b2)² - (b2)². For x² + 6x, the grid shows the extra 9 to take away.

When a is not 1

Expanding a(x + p)² + q and comparing coefficients finds p and q. This works when a is not 1.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
Write x² - 8x + 3 in the form (x + p)² + q.
Halve -8 to get -4, then take away (-4)² = 16.
Halve b(x - 4)² = x² - 8x + 16
Take away 16(x - 4)² - 16 + 3
(x - 4)² - 13
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Write 3x² + 12x - 1 in the form a(x + p)² + q.
Expand a(x + p)² + q and match coefficients.
Expand3(x + p)² + q = 3x² + 6px + 3p² + q
Compare6p = 12
p = 2
12 + q = -1
q = -13
3(x + 2)² - 13
YOUR TURN · c
Write x² + 10x + 7 in the form (x + p)² + q.
(x + 5)² - 25 + 7 = (x + 5)² - 18.
YOUR TURN · d
Write 2x² - 4x + 5 in the form a(x + p)² + q.
2(x + p)² + q = 2x² + 4px + 2p² + q, so p = -1 and q = 3: 2(x - 1)² + 3.
