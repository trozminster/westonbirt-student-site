Trigonometry and Angles of Any Size

Sin, cos and tan began as ratios in right-angled triangles. This lesson tightens that method, then frees the three ratios to work for an angle of any size.

By the end you’ll know…
  • The trigonometric ratios extend beyond right-angled triangles using a point (x,y) on a unit circle, where cos is the x-coordinate and sin the y-coordinate.
  • Angles are measured anticlockwise from the positive x-axis, except bearings, which are clockwise from north.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Label from the angle

The hypotenuse is always opposite the right angle. Opposite and adjacent depend on which angle is marked.

Top or bottom

An unknown on top of the ratio is found by multiplying. An unknown on the bottom is found by dividing.

Elevation and depression

Both angles are measured from the horizontal. The angle of depression equals the angle of elevation back up, as alternate angles.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
In triangle ABC, angle B=90°, AC=12 cm and angle A=35°. Find BC.
BC is opposite A and AC is the hypotenuse, so the ratio is sine.
Opposite and hypotenusesin35°=BC12
Multiply by 12BC=12×sin35°
BC=6.9 cm (1 d.p.)
WORKED EXAMPLE B
In triangle PQR, angle Q=90°, PQ=9 cm and angle P=40°. Find PR.
PQ is adjacent to P and PR is the hypotenuse, so the ratio is cosine. PR is on the bottom, so divide.
Adjacent and hypotenusecos40°=9PR
RearrangePR=9÷cos40°
PR=11.7 cm (1 d.p.)

YOUR TURN · b
From the top of a 60 m cliff, the angle of depression of a boat is 22°. How far is the boat from the foot of the cliff?
The angle of elevation from the boat is also 22°.tan22°=60d, so d=60÷tan22°=148.5m(1 d.p.).

Any angle

An angle is measured anticlockwise from the positive x-axis. Its point on the unit circle is (cos θ, sin θ).

Signs by quadrant

Each ratio takes its sign from the point's coordinates. The table shows which ratios are positive in each quadrant.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
Is cos150° positive or negative?
Place 150° first, then read the sign of x.
Quadrant150° is between 90° and 180°, the second
Sign of xnegative there
cos150° is negative (−0.866)

tanθ=y÷x, so compare the two signs.

YOUR TURN · c
The angle 230° is in which quadrant? Give the signs of sin230°, cos230° and tan230°.
The third quadrant. sin230° and cos230° are negative, and tan230° is positive.
YOUR TURN · d
The point on the unit circle for angle θ is (−0.8,−0.6). Write down cosθ, sinθ and tanθ.
cosθ=−0.8 and sinθ=−0.6, so tanθ=−0.6÷−0.8=0.75
