Rearranging Formulae

A formula is only useful once the letter you need is on its own, and rearranging is just solving an equation with letters instead of numbers - the hard case, where the subject appears twice, is last lesson's factorising put straight to work.

By the end you’ll know…
  • The new subject must end up alone on one side and absent from the other.
  • Where the subject sits inside a square root, square both sides to remove it; where the subject itself is squared, take the square root at the end, allowing for both the positive and negative root where the context needs it.
  • Where the subject appears twice, its terms are collected together and factorised out before dividing.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Undo in reverse

Rearranging undoes the operations on the subject in reverse order. It is solving an equation with letters.

Squares and roots

A square root is removed by squaring both sides. A squared subject needs a root at the end.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
Make r the subject of A=πr2
r is a length here, so take the positive root.
Divide by πr2=Aπ
Square rootr=Aπ
WORKED EXAMPLE B
Make u the subject of v2 =u2+2as.
Subtract the whole term 2as before taking any root.
Subtract 2asu2=v2−2as
Square rootu=±v2−2as

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Collect the subject

When the subject appears twice, its terms are collected on one side. Then the subject is factorised out.

Divide by the bracket

The whole bracket divides the other side. So x=d−ba−c.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
Make x the subject of ax+3=bx−5
Gather every x term on the left, everything else on the right.
Collectax−bx=−8
Factorisex(a−b)=−8
x=8b−a
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Make y the subject of x=y+2y−3.
Multiply out the fraction first, then collect the y terms.
Multiplyxy−3x=y+2
Collectxy−y=3x+2
y(x−1)=3x+2
y=3x+2x−1
YOUR TURN · c
Make p the subject of 5p−q=rp+2
Hint. Follow the worked example: Collect → Factorise
5p−rp =q+2, so p(5−r) =q+2 and p=q+25−r.
YOUR TURN · d
Make t the subject of s =2t+1t−4.
Hint. Follow the worked example: Multiply → Collect
st−4s =2t+1, so st−2t =4s+1, t(s−2) =4s+1 and t =4s+1s−2.
