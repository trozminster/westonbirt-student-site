Graphs of Linear Functions

Straight lines are the simplest graphs, and the gradient and intercept of a line are the two ideas the quadratic, exponential and coordinate-geometry work in the next chapters keep returning to.

By the end you’ll know…
  • To 'draw' or 'plot' a graph means calculating and plotting points accurately, whereas to 'sketch' means showing correct shape and position with axis crossings only.
  • The gradient between two points (x1,y1) and (x2,y2) is y2−y1x2−x1; a horizontal line y=b has gradient zero and a vertical line x = a has an undefined (infinite) gradient.
  • The gradient m and intercept c fix a line y=mx+c completely, and any linear equation can be rearranged into that form or the general form px + qy + r=0

Draw or sketch

Drawing a graph means plotting calculated points accurately. Sketching means showing the shape, with the axis crossings marked.

To draw a graph means to calculate points and plot them accurately, while to sketch a graph means to show its correct shape and position with the axis crossings marked. A sketch needs no scale and no table of values.

Gradient

The gradient is the rise divided by the run. Subtract the y-values and the x-values in the same order.

The gradient is the rise divided by the run between two points, and it is found by subtracting the y-values and the x-values in the same order. Here the rise is 8−2 and the run is 4−1

Zero and undefined

A horizontal line has a gradient of zero. A vertical line's gradient is undefined: you cannot divide by a zero run.

A horizontal line has a rise of zero, so its gradient is zero, and a vertical line has a run of zero, so its gradient is undefined because you cannot divide by zero.

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

y=mx+c

In y=mx+c, m is the gradient and c is the y-intercept. Two numbers fix the line completely.

In y=mx+c the number m is the gradient and c is where the line crosses the y-axis, so two numbers fix the line completely. For y=2x+1 the line starts at 1 on the y-axis and rises 2 for every 1 across.

x=a and y=b

The line x = a is vertical, through a on the x-axis. The line y=b is horizontal, through b on the y-axis.

The equation x = a gives a vertical line through the x-axis at a, and y=b gives a horizontal line through the y-axis at b. The letter in the equation names the axis that is crossed in the other direction.

px + qy + r=0

The form px + qy + r=0 hides m and c. Making y the subject reveals them. Moving a term across the equals sign changes its sign.

An equation in the form px + qy + r=0 is rearranged to make y the subject, and then m and c can be read off. The sign of the x-term changes as it moves across the equals sign.
