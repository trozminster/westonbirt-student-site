Lines and Their Intersections

Finding the equation of a line, and where two lines meet, are the basic moves of coordinate geometry, and they combine with symmetry and area to make problems about triangles.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Two lines intersect where their equations are simultaneously satisfied.
  • A line of symmetry of an isosceles triangle passes through the apex and the midpoint of the base.

Choosing the form

A point and a gradient go straight into y - y1 = m(x - x1). With two points, the gradient comes first. Either point then gives the same line.

Use y - y1 = m(x - x1) when you know a point and the gradient. With two points, find the gradient first and then use either point. Both give the same line.

Symmetry in an isosceles triangle

The line of symmetry runs from the apex to the midpoint of the base. So that midpoint is found first.

The line of symmetry of an isosceles triangle passes through the apex and the midpoint of the base. Find that midpoint first, then the equation of the line through it and the apex.
WORKED EXAMPLE A
Find the equation of the line through (4, -1) with gradient 2.Substitute the point and gradient into y - y1 = m(x - x1).
y - (-1) = 2(x - 4), so y + 1 = 2x - 8
Subtract 1 from both sides
y = 2x - 9
WORKED EXAMPLE B
Find the equation of the line through (1, 7) and (4, 1).Find the gradient first, then use one of the points.
Gradient = 1 - 74 - 1 = -2
y - 7 = -2(x - 1) gives y = -2x + 2 + 7
y = -2x + 9

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Where two lines meet

At the point of intersection both equations are true. So they are solved simultaneously, then checked in both.

At the point of intersection both equations are true, so solve them simultaneously and then find the other coordinate. Check in both equations.

Where a line crosses the axes

To find the x-intercept set y = 0. To find the y-intercept set x = 0.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
Find where y = 3x - 2 and 2x + y = 13 meet.Substitute one equation into the other.
2x + (3x - 2) = 13, so 5x = 15 and x = 3
y = 3(3) - 2 = 7
The lines meet at (3, 7)
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Find where 4x - 3y = 24 crosses the axes, and the area of the triangle it makes with the axes.Set each variable to zero in turn.
y = 0: 4x = 24, so x = 6. x = 0: -3y = 24, so y = -8
The triangle has base 6 and height 8
Area = half x 6 × 8 = 24
YOUR TURN · c
Find where y = 2x + 5 and x + y = 17 meet.
x + (2x + 5) = 17, so 3x = 12 and x = 4. Then y = 2(4) + 5 = 13. The lines meet at (4, 13).
YOUR TURN · d
Find where 5x + 2y = 20 crosses the axes.
y = 0: 5x = 20, so x = 4, giving (4, 0). x = 0: 2y = 20, so y = 10, giving (0, 10).
