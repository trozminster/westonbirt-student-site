Graphs of Quadratic Functions

A quadratic graph is fixed by where its vertex is and which way it opens, and completing the square hands you the vertex directly, so this lesson turns an algebra skill from Chapter 2 into a picture you can sketch in a minute.

By the end you’ll know…
  • The sign of a determines whether a parabola y =ax2+bx+c has a minimum (a>0) or a maximum (a<0).
  • Completing the square to a(x−h)2+k gives the vertex directly as (h,k), with line of symmetry x=h.

02

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

The vertex form

In a(x−h)2 +k, the vertex is (h,k). The line of symmetry is x=h. The bracket shifts the graph right by h.

When a quadratic is written as a(x−h)2 +k, its vertex is at (h,k) and its line of symmetry is x=h. The bracket shifts the graph right by h, and k shifts it up.

Minimum or maximum

The sign of the x2 term decides the vertex type. A positive term opens upwards to a minimum. A negative term opens downwards to a maximum.

The sign of the x2 term decides whether the vertex is a minimum or a maximum. If it is positive the curve opens upwards, and if it is negative the curve opens downwards.

Completing the square

Completing the square starts by halving b. The bracket adds that half squared. So it has to be taken away again.

To write x2+bx+c as (x+b2)2 +q, halve b and then take away the square of that half. The bracket adds the square, so it has to come off again.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

The intercepts

The y-intercept comes from putting x=0
The x-intercepts come from putting y=0 and solving.

The y-intercept is found by putting x=0, and the x-intercepts by putting y=0 and solving, which the completed-square form does neatly. For y =(x−3)2−4 the curve crosses the x-axis at 1 and 5 and the y-axis at 5

A downward curve

A negative x2 term turns the curve upside down. Its vertex is then the highest point.

When the x2 term is negative, the sketch opens downwards and the vertex is the highest point. For y =4−(x−1)2 the maximum is at (1,4) and the curve crosses the x-axis at −1 and 3
