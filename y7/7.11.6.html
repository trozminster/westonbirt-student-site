Topic 7.11 · Solving Equations · Lesson 6 of 6

Unknowns on Both Sides

When the unknown turns up on both sides of the scale, you collect it onto one side before you can undo anything else. Today's equations also come from formulae, with the unknown left standing alone.

By the end you’ll know…
  • When the unknown appears on both sides, the terms in the unknown are collected onto one side first.
  • Moving the smaller unknown term avoids a negative coefficient.
  • An unknown in a denominator is cleared by multiplying both sides by that denominator.
  • Substituting known values into a formula leaves an equation in the remaining unknown, solved the same way.
By the end you can
G5Solve equations with the unknown on both sides, including with brackets.
G5Substitute known values into a formula and solve the resulting equation for the remaining unknown.
