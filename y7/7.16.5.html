Fractions, Decimals, Percentages

The same amount can be written three ways: a half, 0.5 or 50%. Today you convert between fractions, decimals and percentages in every direction.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A decimal converts to a fraction by reading its place value, so 0.7 is 710 and 0.25 is 25100
  • A fraction converts to a decimal by dividing the numerator by the denominator.
  • Multiplying a decimal by 100 gives a percentage, and dividing a percentage by 100 gives a decimal.
  • A percentage converts to a fraction by writing it over 100 and simplifying.
  • The key equivalents are 1÷2=0.5=50%, 1÷4=0.25=25%, 1÷5=0.2=20%, 1÷10=0.1=10%, 3÷4=0.75=75%.
  • A denominator that is not a factor of a power of ten gives a recurring decimal, shown with a dot.

Percentages are hundredths

Multiply a decimal by 100 to get a percentage. Write a percentage over 100 and simplify to get a fraction.

Per cent means per hundred, so multiplying a decimal by 100 gives a percentage. A percentage converts to a fraction by writing it over 100 and simplifying.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Dividing to convert

Divide the numerator by the denominator, writing zeros after the point. So three eighths is 3÷8=0.375

A fraction becomes a decimal by dividing the numerator by the denominator, with zeros written after the point. So the fraction 38 is 3÷8, which is 0.375

Recurring decimals

If the denominator is not a factor of a power of ten, a digit repeats. So 13 is 0.3 recurring.

When the denominator is not a factor of a power of ten, the division never ends and a digit repeats. The fraction 13 is 0.333..., which is called 0.3 recurring.
WORKED EXAMPLE C
Write 58 as a decimal.
58 means 5÷8
each remainder carries to the next digit
Fill with zeros5÷8=5.000÷8
Ones5÷8=0 remainder 5
Tenths50÷8=6 remainder 2
Hundredths20÷8=2 remainder 4
Thousandths40÷8=5
58=0.625
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Write 29 as a decimal.
29 means 2÷9
each remainder carries to the next digit
Fill with zeros2÷9=2.000÷9
Ones2÷9=0 remainder 2
Tenths20÷9=2 remainder 2
Hundredths20÷9=2 remainder 2
again and againthe remainder is always 2, so the digit 2 repeats for ever
29=0.222...
which is 0.2 recurring
