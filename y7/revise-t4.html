Which value disproves the statement 'n squared is always greater than n'?

What can be concluded when 'the product of two primes is always odd' is tested with 3×5=15, 5×7=35 and 2×3=6?
Yes. 2 is prime and even, so 2×3=6 is even. One counter-example makes it false.
Not quite. "Always" fails if even one case fails. 2×3=6 is even, so the statement is false.
