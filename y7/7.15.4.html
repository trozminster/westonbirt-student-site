Coordinates and Straight Lines

Every map, screen and game places points with two numbers. Today you use coordinates in all four quadrants and meet your first lines with an equation.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A coordinate pair gives horizontal position then vertical position, (x, y), across all four quadrants.
  • The origin is (0,0), and negative coordinates lie left of it or below it.
  • A vertical line has equation x = a, because every point on it has the same x-coordinate.
  • A horizontal line has equation y=b, for the same reason.
  • The y-axis is the line x=0 and the x-axis is the line y=0

Across, then up or down

A coordinate pair (x, y) gives the horizontal position first, then the vertical one. Both are measured from the origin (0,0), where the axes cross.

Negative coordinates

A negative x-coordinate lies left of the origin. A negative y-coordinate lies below it. The axes split the grid into four quadrants.

A negative x-coordinate lies left of the origin and a negative y-coordinate lies below it. The two axes split the grid into four quadrants.

A vertical line, x=a

Every point on a vertical line has the same x-coordinate. Its equation is x = that number. The y-axis is x=0
