Topic 7.18 · Symmetry and Transformations · Lesson 5 of 6

Describing Transformations

A transformation is only useful if someone else can repeat it from your description. Today you name the transformation and give every detail it needs.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A transformation is fully described only when all its defining information is given.
  • A translation needs its column vector, a reflection needs its mirror line, and a rotation needs centre, angle and direction.
  • One transformation is named, not several, unless the question asks for a combination.
  • The transformation can be identified from what has changed: position, orientation, or the direction the shape faces.
By the end you can

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

What has changed?

A translation keeps a shape facing the same way. A rotation turns it. A reflection flips it to face the other way.

A translation keeps a shape facing the same way, and a rotation turns it. A reflection flips it over, so it faces the other way.

Then find the details

For a reflection, the mirror line lies halfway between each point and its image. For a translation, count from any one point to its image.
