Nets and Surface Area

Every cardboard box starts as a flat sheet. Today you draw solids on isometric paper, sketch the net of a cuboid, and find how much card it needs: its surface area.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A net is the 2D shape that folds up to form a solid.
  • Surface area is the total area of all the faces, so it is measured in square units, not cubic ones.
  • A cuboid has three pairs of identical faces, so its surface area is 2(lw + lh + wh).
  • Isometric paper shows a 3D shape by drawing its edges along three fixed directions.

Unfolding a solid

A net is the flat shape that folds up to form a solid. A cuboid's net has six rectangles, in three identical pairs.

Isometric drawing

On isometric paper the upright edges stay vertical. The lengths and widths follow the two slanted directions of the grid.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
Draw a cuboid 3 by 2 by 2 on isometric paper.
Startthe front vertical edge, 2 squares tall
Bottom3 along one slant and 2 along the other
Uprightthe other two vertical edges, then the top face joins them
WORKED EXAMPLE B
Sketch a net of a cuboid 4 cm by 3 cm by 2 cm.
Basea 4 by 3 rectangle, its edges the fold lines
Sidesa 4 by 2 face on its long edges and a 3 by 2 face on its short edges
Topa 4 by 3 face on one long side face
YOUR TURN · a

On isometric paper, which edges of a cuboid are drawn vertically?
Only its upright edges, the heights; the lengths and widths follow the slants.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Adding the faces

Surface area is the total area of all the faces. A cuboid has three pairs of identical faces. So its surface area is 2(lw + lh + wh).

Surface area is the total area of all the faces, measured in square units. A cuboid has three pairs of identical faces, so its surface area is 2(lw + lh + wh).

Not the volume

Surface area measures the card on the outside, in square units. Volume measures the space inside, in cubic units.

Surface area measures the card on the outside, in square units. Volume measures the space inside, in cubic units, so the two are different numbers.
WORKED EXAMPLE C
Find the surface area of a cube with edges of 4 cm.
Area of one face4 × 4 = 16 cm²
Surface area6 × 16 = 96 cm²
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Find the surface area of a cuboid 6 cm by 4 cm by 3 cm.
Front and back2 × 6 × 4 = 48
Top and bottom2 × 6 × 3 = 36
Two ends2 × 4 × 3 = 24
Surface area48 + 36 + 24 = 108 cm²
YOUR TURN · c
Find the surface area of a cuboid 5 cm by 5 cm by 2 cm.
2 × (25 + 10 + 10) = 90 cm².
YOUR TURN · d
A cube has a surface area of 54 cm². Find the length of its edges.
54 ÷ 6 = 9, so each face is 9 cm² and each edge is 3 cm.
