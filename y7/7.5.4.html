Topic 7.5 · Operations and Negative Numbers · Lesson 4 of 6

Multiplying and Dividing Negatives

Yesterday you added and subtracted negative numbers. Today you multiply and divide them - and the rule for the sign of the answer is simpler than you might expect, but easy to misapply once powers get involved.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Two numbers with the same sign multiply or divide to a positive.
  • Two numbers with different signs give a negative.
  • In a longer product, an odd number of negative factors gives a negative result, an even number a positive.
  • Even powers of a negative are positive, odd powers negative: (-2)² = 4, (-2)³ = -8.

Same signs give positive, different signs give negative

Same signs multiply or divide to a positive answer. Different signs give a negative answer.

Two numbers with the same sign multiply or divide to a positive answer; two numbers with different signs give a negative answer. This applies to both multiplication and division equally.

Checking with a simple case

If unsure, check with a simple known fact. 3 × (-1) = -3, and -3 × (-1) = 3.

If unsure, check with a simple known fact: 3 × (-1) = -3 confirms positive × negative = negative; -3 × (-1) = 3 confirms negative × negative = positive, since multiplying by -1 just flips the sign once.

Longer products and evenodd counts

Count the negative factors in a longer product. An odd number of negatives gives a negative result. An even number gives a positive result.

In a longer product with several negative factors, the sign of the result depends on how many of them are negative - an odd number of negative factors gives a negative result overall, an even number gives a positive result, because each pair of negatives cancels back to positive.

Powers of a negative number

Even powers of a negative are positive. Odd powers of a negative are negative. (-2)² = 4, but (-2)³ = -8.

Even powers of a negative are positive, odd powers are negative: (-2)² = (-2) × (-2) = 4, but (-2)³ = (-2) × (-2) × (-2) = -8. This is the same oddeven counting rule, since a power is just a repeated product of the same negative factor.
