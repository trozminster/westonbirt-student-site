Topic 7.14 · Area of Triangles and Compound Shapes · Lesson 2 of 3

Reverse Area Problems

Builders and designers often know the area they need and must work out a length. Today you run the area formulas backwards to find a missing side.

By the end you’ll know…
  • An area formula can be applied backwards, using inverse operations to find a missing length.
  • For a triangle, base =2 × area / height.
  • For a rectangle, a missing side is the area divided by the known side.
  • Where the missing length appears more than once, the situation is written as an equation and solved.

Running a formula backwards

Run an area formula backwards to find a missing length. For a triangle, base = area × 2 ÷ height.

An area formula can be run backwards to find a missing length, undoing each step in reverse order. For a triangle, doubling the area and dividing by the height gives the base.

A rectangle's missing side

A rectangle's missing side is its area divided by the known side.

A rectangle's area is length × width, so a missing side is the area divided by the known side. Division undoes the multiplication.
WORKED EXAMPLE A
A triangle has area 24 cm² and perpendicular height 6 cm. Find its base.
Write the formula12×base×6=24
Multiply both sides by 2base × 6=48
Divide by 6base=8 cm
WORKED EXAMPLE B
A triangle has area 35 cm² and base 10 cm. Find its perpendicular height.
Write the formula12×10×height=35
Multiply both sides by 210 × height=70
Divide by 10height=7 cm

Working back from a perimeter

Take the two known sides off the perimeter. Share what is left between the two missing sides.

A rectangle's perimeter counts both lengths and both widths. Taking the two known sides off the perimeter leaves the two equal missing sides, so what is left is shared between them.

When the unknown repeats

When a missing length repeats, write the formula as an equation. Solve it to find the length.

Where a missing length appears more than once, or inside an expression, the formula is written as an equation. Solving the equation finds the length.
WORKED EXAMPLE C
A rectangle has perimeter 34 cm and length 11 cm. Find its width.
Write the perimeter2×11+2 × width=34
22+2 × width=34
Subtract 22 from both sides2 × width=12
Divide by 2width=6 cm
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Find x when a triangle with base 2x cm and perpendicular height 6 cm has area 42 cm²
Write the equation12×2x×6=42
Simplify the left side6x=42
Divide both sides by 6x=7
