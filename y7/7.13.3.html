Area and Perimeter of Rectangles

Perimeter and area answer two different questions about the same shape - how far around it, and how much space it covers. Today you learn to find both for rectangles and shapes made of rectangles.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Perimeter is the distance around a shape, measured in units of length.
  • Area is the space a shape covers, measured in square units.
  • The area of a rectangle is length × width, and its perimeter is twice the length plus twice the width.
  • All lengths must be in the same unit before either is calculated.
  • A compound rectangular shape is split into rectangles whose areas are added.

Perimeter and area, kept separate

Perimeter is the distance around a shape, in units of length. Area is the space it covers, in square units.

Perimeter is the distance around a shape, measured in units of length; area is the space a shape covers, measured in square units. The two answer different questions and are never the same kind of quantity.

The rectangle formulas

Area of a rectangle = length × width. Perimeter = twice the length plus twice the width. Use the same unit for all lengths first.

The area of a rectangle is length × width; its perimeter is twice the length plus twice the width, since a rectangle has two pairs of equal sides. All lengths must be in the same unit before either is calculated.

Working backwards to a missing side

Reverse the formula to find a missing side. Divide the area by the known side. Or halve the perimeter, then subtract the known side.

Given a rectangle's area or perimeter and one side, the missing side is found by reversing the formula - dividing the area by the known side, or halving the perimeter before subtracting the known side.

Compound rectangular shapes

Split a compound shape into non-overlapping rectangles. Find each area, then add them.

A shape made of rectangles is split into non-overlapping rectangular pieces that together cover the whole shape exactly once; each piece's area is found separately, then the areas are added.
WORKED EXAMPLE C
A rectangle has area 48 cm² and length 8 cm. Find its width.
Reverse the formulawidth = area ÷ length
Substitute=48÷8
=6 cm
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Find the area of this L-shape, split into rectangles A and B.
Area A8×3=24 cm²
Area B5×2=10 cm²
Total area24+10=34 cm²
