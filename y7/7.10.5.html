Dividing Fractions

How many 34 m lengths of ribbon can be cut from 6 m? The answer is more than 6 - and today explains why dividing by a fraction can make a number bigger.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal.
  • This works because dividing asks how many of the divisor fit into the dividend.
  • Dividing by a fraction less than 1 gives a larger answer.
  • A whole number is written over 1 before dividing.

How many fit

Dividing asks how many of the divisor fit into the dividend. Dividing by a fraction less than 1 gives a bigger answer.

Dividing asks how many of the divisor fit into the dividend. Dividing by a fraction less than 1 gives a bigger answer, because a small piece fits many times.

Multiply by the reciprocal

Dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal. A whole number is written over 1 first.

Spotting a division

How many pieces fit into a total is a division. Divide the total by the size of one piece.

A question asking how many pieces of one size fit into a total is a division. The total is divided by the size of one piece.

Checking the size

Dividing by a fraction less than 1 gives more than the total. A smaller answer means the division was set up wrongly.

Dividing by a fraction less than 1 gives an answer bigger than the total. An answer smaller than the total means the division was set up the wrong way round.
