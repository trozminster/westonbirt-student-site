Percentages of an Amount

Sales, tips and pay rises are all percentages of an amount. Today you find them in your head, by building any percentage from four easy blocks.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Per cent means per hundred, so a percentage is a fraction out of 100
  • 50% is found by dividing by 2, 25% by dividing by 4, 10% by dividing by 10, and 1% by dividing by 100
  • Any percentage can be built by adding or scaling these blocks, so 35% is 25%+10%.
  • An increase adds the percentage to the original amount; a decrease subtracts it.
By the end you can

Per hundred

Per cent means per hundred: a percentage is a fraction out of 100
50% is a half, 25% a quarter, 10% a tenth, 1% a hundredth.

Per cent means per hundred, so a percentage is a fraction out of 100
50% is a half, 25% a quarter, 10% a tenth and 1% a hundredth.

The four blocks

Each block is one division of the whole amount. 50% divides by 2, 25% by 4, 10% by 10 and 1% by 100

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Building any percentage

Any percentage can be built by adding or scaling the four blocks. 35% is 25%+10%, and 5% is half of 10%.

Increase and decrease

An increase adds the percentage on. A decrease takes it away. The answer is the new amount, not the change.

An increase adds the percentage onto the original amount, and a decrease takes it away. The answer is the new amount, not the change.
WORKED EXAMPLE C
Find 35% of 260
Find 25%260÷4=65
Find 10%260÷10=26
Add for 35%65+26=91
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Decrease 80 kg by 15%.
Find 10%80÷10=8 kg
Halve for 5%8÷2=4 kg
Add for 15%8+4=12 kg
Subtract the decrease80−12=68 kg
