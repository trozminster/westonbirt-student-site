9×4=36 m

Why measure from the ruler's zero, not its end?
Many rulers have a gap before 0, so starting at the end adds length that isn't there.
Exit ticket · 7.13.1
Explain why a length must be measured from a ruler's zero mark rather than its physical end.
The physical end may be worn, chipped, or printed with a border before the zero mark, so measuring from it would give the wrong length.
