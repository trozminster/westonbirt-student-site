Topic 7.14 · Area of Triangles and Compound Shapes · Lesson 3 of 3

Compound Shapes

Floor plans, gardens and signs are rarely a single rectangle. Today you find the area and perimeter of shapes built from rectangles and triangles, and use algebra when a side is unknown.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A compound shape is split into rectangles and triangles whose areas are added.
  • It can instead be treated as a surrounding rectangle with a missing piece subtracted.
  • Perimeter adds the outside lengths only, and missing lengths are deduced from the ones given.
  • Algebraic side lengths give an expression for area or perimeter, which becomes an equation once its value is stated.
By the end you can

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Splitting into pieces

Split a compound shape into rectangles and triangles, then add the areas. Find any missing length from the lengths given.

A compound shape's area is found by splitting it into rectangles and triangles and adding their areas. Any missing length comes from the lengths already given.

Taking a piece away

The same shape can be seen as a surrounding rectangle with a piece missing. Its area is then the big rectangle's area minus the missing piece.

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Around the outside

A perimeter adds the outside lengths only, never a line inside the shape. A missing outside length is found from the lengths given on the opposite side.

Algebra on the sides

With an algebraic side, the perimeter is an expression. Given its value, solve the equation for the unknown.

With an algebraic side length, the area or the perimeter is an expression. It becomes an equation once its value is given, and solving the equation finds the unknown.
WORKED EXAMPLE C
Find the perimeter of this shape.
Missing width9−5=4
Missing height7−4=3
Add all six sides9 +4 +4 +3 +5 +7
Perimeter=32 cm
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Find x when a rectangle of width 5 cm and length (2x+1) cm has area 45 cm².
Write the equation5(2x+1)=45
Expand the bracket10x+5=45
Subtract 5 from both sides10x=40
Divide by 10x=4
