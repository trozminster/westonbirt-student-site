Finding a Missing Length

With one angle and one side, trigonometry finds any other side of a right-angled triangle. Surveyors and pilots use it every day.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Once the ratio is chosen, the unknown side is found by rearranging the ratio: from sin x = opposite / hypotenuse, opposite = hypotenuse x sin x and hypotenuse = opposite ÷ sin x.
  • Whether the unknown is multiplied or divided depends on whether it is on the top or the bottom of the ratio.
  • An answer is checked against the triangle: the hypotenuse must be the longest side.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Rearrange the ratio

Rearranged, sin x = opposite ÷ hypotenuse gives opposite = hypotenuse × sin x. So here x=10 × sin 30°=5 cm.

From sin x = opposite ÷ hypotenuse, the opposite side is the hypotenuse × sin x. With a hypotenuse of 10 cm and an angle of 30°, the opposite side is 10 × sin 30°=5 cm.

On the top or on the bottom

When the unknown side is on the top of the ratio, multiply. When it is on the bottom, divide, as in hypotenuse = opposite ÷ sin x.

Check against the hypotenuse

The hypotenuse is always the longest side. An answer that makes another side longer than the hypotenuse is wrong.

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Draw the triangle first

A problem in words is turned into a right-angled triangle before any ratio is chosen. A ladder, a wall and the ground make one, with the ladder as the hypotenuse.

North and east make a right angle

A bearing is measured clockwise from north, and north and east are at right angles. So a journey on a bearing splits into a distance north and a distance east.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
A 6 m ladder leans on a wall at 70° to the ground. How high does it reach, to 1 decimal place?
The ladder is the hypotenuse and the height is opposite 70°.
Write the ratiosin 70°=h6
Multiply both sides by 6h=6 × sin 70°
h=5.638...
Round to 1 d.p.h=5.6 m
WORKED EXAMPLE D
A boat sails 10 km on a bearing of 060°. How far east has it gone, to 1 decimal place?
North and east meet at a right angle, with 60° at the start.
Write the ratiosin 60°=e10
Multiply both sides by 10e=10 × sin 60°
e=8.660...
Round to 1 d.p.e=8.7 km
YOUR TURN · c

YOUR TURN · d
