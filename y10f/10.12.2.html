Scale Drawings and Maps

A map shrinks a whole county onto one sheet, and an architect draws a house before it is built. Both work only because every length is shrunk by the same scale.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A scale of 1 : n means that 1 unit on the drawing stands for n of the same units in real life.
  • A map scale of 1 : 25000 means 1 cm on the map is 25000 cm, which is 250 m, in real life.
  • Real length = drawing length x n, and drawing length = real length ÷ n, so the two are inverse operations.
By the end you can
G3Use a given scale to convert between a length on a map or scale drawing and the real length it stands for.
G4Produce a scale drawing to a stated scale and use it to answer a question about a real distance.

Learn

One unit stands for n

A scale of 1 : n means 1 drawing unit is n real units. So on a 1 : 25,000 map, 1 cm is 250 m.

A scale of 1 : n means 1 unit on the drawing stands for n of the same unit in real life. On a 1 : 25,000 map, 1 cm stands for 25,000 cm, which is 250 m.

Multiply out, divide back

Real length = drawing length × n, and drawing length = real length ÷ n. The two are inverse operations, and the units match before either is used.

WORKED EXAMPLE A

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Every length, the same scale

A scale drawing divides every real length by the same n. So it is the same shape as the real thing. Angles are not scaled.

A scale drawing divides every real length by the same n, so it is the same shape as the real thing. The angles are not scaled: a right angle stays a right angle.

Convert, draw, measure

Convert each real length first. Draw from the 0 on the ruler. Multiply a measured length by n to get the real length.

Convert each real length first, then draw from the 0 on the ruler. A length measured on the finished drawing is multiplied by n to give the real length.
WORKED EXAMPLE C
At 1 : 200, draw a 12 m by 7 m garden.
The garden is a rectangle. 12m=1200 cm and 1200÷200=6 cm. 7m=700 cm and 700÷200=3.5 cm. Draw from 0 on the ruler, with right angles at the corners.
Drawing6 cm by 3.5 cm
WORKED EXAMPLE D
At 1 : 5000, find the field's longest side.
The field is a right-angled triangle with shorter sides 240 m and 100 m. 24,000÷5000=4.8 cm and 10,000÷5000=2 cm, at right angles. The longest side measures 5.2 cm.
Real length5.2×5000=26,000 cm =260 m
