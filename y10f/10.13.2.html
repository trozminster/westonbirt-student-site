Calculating with Fractions

Recipes, shares and times are often given as fractions, and a calculator shows a decimal that may not be exact. Working exactly with fractions keeps the answer exact.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Fractions are added or subtracted only over a common denominator; the numerators are then added or subtracted and the denominator is unchanged.
  • To multiply fractions, multiply the numerators and multiply the denominators.
  • To divide by a fraction, multiply by its reciprocal, because a unit fraction is a multiplicative inverse: 3 ÷ (1 ÷ 5) = 3 × 5.
  • A mixed number is converted to an improper fraction before multiplying or dividing.
By the end you can
G3Add, subtract, multiply and divide fractions and mixed numbers, giving each answer in its simplest form.
G4Calculate exactly with fractions in context, finding a fraction of a quantity and expressing one quantity as a fraction of another.

A common denominator first

Fractions are added or subtracted only over a common denominator. Then the numerators are added and the denominator stays the same.

Multiply straight across

To multiply fractions, multiply the numerators and multiply the denominators, with no common denominator needed. A mixed number is made improper first.

Divide by multiplying

Dividing by a fraction means multiplying by its reciprocal. So 3 ÷ 15 = 3 × 5 = 15. Every whole holds 5 fifths.

Dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal. So 3 ÷ 15 = 3 × 5 = 15, because every whole holds 5 fifths.

The part over the whole

One quantity as a fraction of another is part over whole. Use the same units: 20 minutes of 2 hours is 20120 = 16.

One quantity as a fraction of another is the part over the whole, in the same units. So 20 minutes of 2 hours is 20120 = 16.
