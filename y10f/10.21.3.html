Speed, Density and Pressure

A best buy compares price per gram. Speed is distance per hour, density is mass per cubic centimetre and pressure is force per square metre: three more 'per' measures, each worked with the same three-way relationship.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A compound measure combines two other measures into a single one.
  • Its units state the relationship: km/h is kilometres divided by hours, so speed = distance / time.
  • Rearranging gives distance = speed × time and time = distance / speed.
  • Density is mass divided by volume, in g/cm³ or kg/m³, and rearranges the same way.
  • Pressure is force divided by area, in N/m² or N/cm², and rearranges the same way; the exam gives its formula.
  • The units are the check: if they do not come out right, the operation was the wrong one.

The unit says how to calculate

A compound measure's unit says how to find it: km/h is km ÷ hours. So speed=distancetime, which rearranges two more ways.

A compound measure combines two measures in one, and its unit says how: km/h is kilometres divided by hours. So speed=distancetime, and the same three quantities rearrange two more ways.

Time in hours, not minutes

A speed in km/h needs the time in hours: divide minutes by 60
1 hour 15 minutes is 1.25 hours, not 1.15

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Density is mass per unit of volume

Density is mass per cubic centimetre: density=massvolume, in g/cm³
It rearranges exactly as speed does.

Density says how much mass is packed into each cubic centimetre, so density=massvolume, in g/cm³ or kg/m³
It rearranges exactly as speed does.

Pressure is force per unit of area

Pressure is force divided by area, in N/m², and the exam gives the formula. The same force on a smaller area makes a greater pressure.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
A block of metal has density 8 g/cm³ and volume 25 cm³
Find its mass.
Rearrangemass = density × volume
Mass8×25=200 g
WORKED EXAMPLE D
A box pushes down with a force of 600 N on a face 0.4 m by 0.5 m. Find the pressure.
Area0.4×0.5=0.2 m²
Pressure600÷0.2=3000 N/m²
YOUR TURN · c

Find its density.
270÷100=2.7 g/cm³
YOUR TURN · d
