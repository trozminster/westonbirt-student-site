Listing Outcomes to Find Probabilities

Games, weather forecasts and insurance all rest on how likely things are. Today we find probabilities by listing every possible outcome.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A probability is a number from 0 to 1, where 0 means impossible, 1 means certain and 12 means an even chance.
  • When outcomes are equally likely, P(event) = (number of outcomes in the event) / (total number of outcomes).
  • A sample space is the list of every possible outcome, and for two successive events it is listed systematically, for example in a grid, so that none is missed or counted twice.
  • The probabilities of all the possible outcomes add up to 1, so P(not A)=1−P(A).
