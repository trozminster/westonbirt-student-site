Topic 10.1 · Number Foundations · Lesson 2 of 6

Directed Numbers

Temperatures, bank balances and sea-level heights all use negative numbers - getting the sign wrong here doesn't just cost a mark, it flips the meaning of the answer.

By the end you’ll know…
  • Adding a positive number moves right on a number line and adding a negative number moves left; subtracting works the opposite way, so subtracting a negative has the same effect as adding a positive.
  • When multiplying or dividing directed numbers, work out the size of the answer first, then decide its sign separately: same signs (both positive or both negative) give a positive answer, different signs give a negative answer.

Adding a positive moves right on a number line. Adding a negative moves left. Subtracting a negative moves right, like adding.

On a number line, adding a positive number moves right and adding a negative number moves left. Subtracting works the opposite way: subtracting a positive moves left and subtracting a negative moves right - so subtracting a negative has the same effect as adding its positive.

Count 5 left=−8

Write as addition=−2+6
Count 6 right=4

Work out the size and the sign separately. Same signs give a positive answer. Different signs give a negative answer.

When multiplying or dividing directed numbers, the answer's size and its sign are worked out separately: same signs (both positive or both negative) give a positive answer; different signs give a negative answer.
