Topic 10.8 · Forming and Solving Equations · Lesson 3 of 5

Unknown on Both Sides

Comparing two phone plans or two taxi fares puts the unknown on both sides at once. Collecting it on one side turns the problem back into one you can already solve.

By the end you’ll know…
  • When the letter appears on both sides, the terms in the letter are collected onto one side and the numbers onto the other.
  • Adding or subtracting the same term from both sides is the same balancing move as adding or subtracting a number.
  • Moving the smaller term in the letter keeps its coefficient positive, which avoids a negative coefficient to handle at the end.

Learn

Gather the letters on one side

When the letter is on both sides, a letter term is subtracted from both sides. That is the same balancing move as subtracting a number.

Move the smaller letter term

Subtracting the smaller letter term keeps the coefficient positive. The numbers then go to the other side.

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Expand, then gather

Expand the bracket first. Every other term stays where it is. Then gather the letters on one side.

When a bracket sits on one side, it is expanded first and every other term stays where it is. The letters are then gathered on one side as before.

The answer is the letter

The equation is finished only when the letter stands on its own. A value for 3x is not yet the answer.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
Solve 4(x+3) =x+27
Write the equation4(x+3)=x+27
Expand the bracket4x+12=x+27
Subtract x from both sides3x+12=27
Subtract 123x=15
Divide by 3x=5
