Topic 10.7 · Angles and Polygons · Lesson 4 of 4

Interior and Exterior Angles

Honeycombs, floor tiles and road signs are polygons. One fact about triangles gives the angle total of every one of them.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A polygon is a closed shape made of straight sides, named by how many it has: triangle, quadrilateral, pentagon, hexagon, heptagon, octagon.
  • The interior angles of a polygon with n sides add to 180×(n−2) degrees, which the specification also writes as (2n−4) right angles.
  • The exterior angles of any polygon add to 360°, and each interior angle and the exterior angle beside it add to 180°.
  • A regular polygon has all its sides and all its angles equal, so each of its exterior angles is 360n degrees.

Triangles inside a polygon

Diagonals from one corner split a polygon with n sides into n−2 triangles. So its interior angles add to 180×(n−2) degrees.

One full turn round the outside

Walking once round any polygon turns through 360°, so its exterior angles add to 360°. In a regular polygon they are equal, so each one is 360÷n.

Working back to the number of sides

The exterior angles of a regular polygon share 360° equally. So the number of sides is 360 ÷ the exterior angle.

The exterior angles of a regular polygon share 360° equally. So the number of sides is 360 ÷ the exterior angle. From an interior angle, find the exterior angle first: they add to 180°.
