Topic 10.11 · Volume and Surface Area · Lesson 1 of 3

Surface Area of Solids

A box maker pays for the card that wraps a box, and a painter for the paint that covers a tank. Both are paying for surface area.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A solid is named by its shape: cube, cuboid, prism, pyramid, cylinder, sphere and cone; a face is a flat surface, an edge is where two faces meet and a vertex is a corner.
  • A prism has the same cross-section all the way along its length.
  • The surface area of a solid is the total area of all its faces, found by adding the areas of the rectangles and triangles of its net.
  • A cylinder's curved surface area is 2×π×r×h and its two ends are circles, so its total surface area is 2×π×r×h +2×π×r2
  • Surface area is measured in square units.

Learn

The same shape all along

A prism has the same cross-section all along its length. A triangular prism has two triangular ends and three rectangular faces.

Add the areas of every face

Surface area is the total area of all the faces, in square units. A cuboid has three pairs of equal opposite faces.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
Find the surface area of a cuboid 5 cm by 4 cm by 3 cm.
Three faces20, 15 and 12
Find each face5×4=20
5×3=15
4×3=12
Each face twiceA=2×(20+15+12)
=2×47
Surface areaA=94 cm²
WORKED EXAMPLE B
Find the surface area of this prism.
Two triangles2×12×8×6=48
Add the widths8+6+10=24
Three rectangles24×12=288
Surface area48+288=336 cm²

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

The curved surface unrolls

A cylinder's curved surface unrolls into a rectangle. It is 2×π×r wide and h tall. The two circular ends add 2×π×r2

A cylinder's curved surface unrolls into a rectangle, as wide as the circumference 2×π×r and as tall as h. So its area is 2×π×r×h, and the two circular ends add 2×π×r2

A pyramid uses its slant height

A square-based pyramid has a square base and four equal triangles. Each triangle's height is the slant height up the face, not the vertical height inside.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
A cylinder has radius 3 cm and height 10 cm. Find its total surface area to 1 d.p.
Find the curved surface2×π×3×10=188.49...
Square the radius32=9
Find the two ends2×π×9=56.54...
188.49...+56.54...=245.04...
Round to 1 d.p.245.0 cm²
WORKED EXAMPLE D
Find the surface area of this pyramid.
Each triangle uses the slant height 5, not the vertical height 4
Base6×6=36
Four triangles4×12×6×5=60
Surface area36+60=96 cm²
YOUR TURN · c
A cylinder has radius 5 cm and height 8 cm. Find its total surface area to 1 d.p.
Hint. Follow the worked example: Find the curved surface → Square the radius → Find the two ends → Round to 1 d.p.
2×π×5×8 +2×π×52 ≈408.4 cm²
YOUR TURN · d
A square-based pyramid has base edges 10 cm, slant height 13 cm and vertical height 12 cm. Find its surface area.
Hint. Follow the worked example: Base → Four triangles → Surface area
100 +4×12×10×13 =360 cm²
