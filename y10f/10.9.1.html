Topic 10.9 · Pythagoras' Theorem · Lesson 1 of 3

Finding the Hypotenuse

Builders check that a corner is square with a 3, 4, 5 triangle, and a TV is sold by its diagonal. Both rest on one fact about right-angled triangles.

By the end you’ll know…
  • A right-angled triangle has one angle of exactly 90°, and the side opposite that angle is the hypotenuse, which is always its longest side.
  • Pythagoras' theorem says the square drawn on the hypotenuse has the same area as the squares on the two shorter sides added together: a2+b2=c2, where c is the hypotenuse.
  • The theorem is true only for right-angled triangles, so the right angle is found in the diagram before the theorem is used.
  • A length is never negative, so only the positive square root is taken.
By the end you can
G5Calculate the hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle from its two shorter sides, giving the answer to a stated accuracy.
G5Explain why a2+b2=c2 by comparing the areas of the three squares drawn on the sides of a right-angled triangle.

The side opposite the right angle

The hypotenuse is the side opposite the right angle. It is always the longest side. Find it from the right angle, not by eye.

The hypotenuse is the side opposite the right angle, and it is always the longest side. It is found from the right angle, not from how long the sides look.

Square, add, square root

For a right-angled triangle a2+b2=c2, where c is the hypotenuse. So the hypotenuse is the square root of the two shorter sides squared and added.

Squares on the sides

Draw a square on each side of a right-angled triangle. The two smaller squares together have exactly the area of the square on the hypotenuse.

Areas add, lengths do not

The theorem adds the areas of the squares, not the lengths. It holds only in a right-angled triangle.

The theorem adds the areas of the squares, so the side lengths themselves do not add. It holds only when the triangle has a right angle.
WORKED EXAMPLE C
The squares on the shorter sides have areas 25 cm² and 144 cm². Find the square on the hypotenuse, and the hypotenuse.
Square on the hypotenuse25+144=169 cm²
Hypotenuse169=13 cm
