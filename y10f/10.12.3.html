Converting Area and Volume Units

Flooring is priced per square metre and a tank is measured in centimetres but filled in litres. Changing between these units is where most errors are made.

By the end you’ll know…
  • 1 cm = 10 mm, 1 m = 100 cm and 1 km = 1000 m.
  • Area has two length dimensions, so the conversion factor is squared: 1 cm² = 10² mm2 = 100 mm² and 1 m² = 100² cm2 = 10000 cm².
  • Volume has three, so it is cubed: 1 cm³ = 10³ mm3 = 1000 mm³ and 1 m³ = 100³ cm3 = 1000000 cm³.
  • 1 litre = 1000 cm³.

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Area squares the factor

1 m = 100 cm, so a square metre is 100 cm by 100 cm. Its area is 100 × 100 = 10,000 cm², not 100 cm².

Volume cubes the factor

For area, the length factor is squared. For volume, it is cubed. So 1 m³ = 100³ = 1,000,000 cm³.

Area has two lengths, so the factor is squared; volume has three, so it is cubed. So 1 m³ = 100³ = 1,000,000 cm³, and 1 litre = 1000 cm³.

05

Learn

Read each card, then follow the worked examples one step at a time.

Convert before you calculate

Put every length in the same unit before working out an area or volume. So 2 m by 40 cm becomes 200 cm by 40 cm.

Every length must be in the same unit before an area or a volume is worked out. A rectangle 2 m by 40 cm is 200 cm by 40 cm, so its area is 8000 cm².

Finish in the unit asked for

Work in one unit, then convert the answer at the end. A capacity uses 1 litre = 1000 cm³.
