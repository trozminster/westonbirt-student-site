Properties of Quadrilaterals

Windows, kites, tiles and ramps are all quadrilaterals, and each family has its own rules. Knowing the rules names a shape and finds its missing angles.

By the end you’ll know…
  • The angles of any quadrilateral sum to 360°.
  • A parallelogram has two pairs of parallel sides, equal opposite sides and equal opposite angles; a rhombus is a parallelogram with all four sides equal.
  • A rectangle is a parallelogram with four right angles, and a square is both a rectangle and a rhombus.
  • A kite has two pairs of adjacent equal sides and one pair of equal opposite angles; a trapezium has exactly one pair of parallel sides.

Parallelograms and rhombuses

A parallelogram has two pairs of parallel sides. Its opposite sides and opposite angles are equal. A rhombus is a parallelogram with four equal sides.

A parallelogram has two pairs of parallel sides, with opposite sides equal and opposite angles equal. A rhombus is a parallelogram whose four sides are all equal.

Rectangles and squares

A rectangle is a parallelogram with four right angles. A square is both a rectangle and a rhombus.

A rectangle is a parallelogram with four right angles. A square is both a rectangle and a rhombus: four right angles and four equal sides.

Kites and trapeziums

A kite has two pairs of equal sides next to each other. A kite has one pair of equal opposite angles. A trapezium has exactly one pair of parallel sides.

A kite has two pairs of equal sides next to each other, and one pair of equal opposite angles. A trapezium has exactly one pair of parallel sides.

The angles add to 360°

A diagonal splits any quadrilateral into two triangles. So its angles add up to 2×180°=360°.

Use the shape's own facts

In a parallelogram, opposite angles are equal and neighbouring angles add to 180°. Give the property as the reason for each step.

Co-interior angles add up to 180°x=180−65
=115°
