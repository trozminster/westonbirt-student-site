Solving Linear Inequalities

Many limits in life are not exact: at least 18, no more than 40 people, under 2 kg. Today we solve inequalities, whose answers are whole sets of numbers.

By the end you’ll know…
  • The symbols are: < is less than, > is greater than, ≤ is less than or equal to, and ≥ is greater than or equal to.
  • An inequality is solved by the same balancing moves as an equation, but the answer is a SET of values rather than a single value.
  • Multiplying or dividing both sides by a negative number reverses the direction of the inequality sign.
  • A solution is checked by substituting one value from inside the set and one from outside it.

Four symbols

A negative reverses the sign

Multiplying or dividing both sides by a negative number reverses the inequality sign. The reason is that 2<5, but −2>−5

Or keep the letter positive

Or add a term to both sides to keep the letter positive. Then no division by a negative is needed. Both routes give x>−2

Adding a term to both sides can make the letter's term positive, so no division by a negative is needed. Both routes give the same set.
