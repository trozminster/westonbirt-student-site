Naming Sides and Choosing a Ratio

Trigonometry finds lengths and angles that cannot be measured directly, such as the height of a cliff. It starts with naming the sides of a right-angled triangle.

By the end you’ll know…
  • In a right-angled triangle the sides are named relative to a chosen angle: the hypotenuse is opposite the right angle, the opposite side faces the chosen angle and the adjacent side is between the chosen angle and the right angle.
  • sinx=opposite / hypotenuse, cosx=adjacent / hypotenuse and tanx=opposite / adjacent.
  • The ratio to use is the one whose two sides are the side known and the side wanted.
  • The calculator must be in degrees.

The hypotenuse

In a right-angled triangle the hypotenuse is the longest side, opposite the right angle. It stays the same whichever angle is chosen.

Opposite and adjacent

The opposite side faces the chosen angle. The adjacent side lies between that angle and the right angle. They are shortened to H, O and A.

The opposite side faces the chosen angle. The adjacent side is next to it, between the chosen angle and the right angle. The three are shortened to H, O and A.

A new angle, new names

Choosing the other angle swaps the opposite and adjacent sides. The hypotenuse stays where it is.

WORKED EXAMPLE A
Name the sides for the 35° angle.
SidesH=PR
O=QR
A=PQ
WORKED EXAMPLE B
Name the sides for the 50° angle.
SidesH=LN
O=MN
A=LM

Three ratios

The three ratios are sin, cos and tan. For a given angle, each ratio is a fixed number.

The three ratios are sinx=opposite÷hypotenuse, cosx=adjacent÷hypotenuse and tanx=opposite÷adjacent. For a given angle, each is a fixed number.

Known and wanted

The side you know and the side you want decide the ratio. The ratio that uses exactly those two sides is the one to use.

WORKED EXAMPLE C
The hypotenuse is 12 cm and the side opposite the 40° angle is wanted. Which ratio?
Sidesopposite (wanted) and hypotenuse (known)
Ratiosin, as sin = opposite ÷ hypotenuse
WORKED EXAMPLE D
The side adjacent to 55° is 8 cm and the opposite side is wanted. Which ratio?
Sidesopposite (wanted) and adjacent (known)
Ratiotan, as tan = opposite ÷ adjacent
