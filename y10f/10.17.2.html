Expanding Double Brackets

Two brackets multiplied together make a quadratic, the shape behind a thrown ball or a curved bridge. Today every term meets every term.

By the end you’ll know…
  • To expand (x+a)(x+b) every term in the first bracket multiplies every term in the second, giving four products.
  • Collecting the two x terms gives x2 +(a+b)x +ab, so the x coefficient is the SUM of a and b and the constant is their PRODUCT.
  • (x+a)2 means (x+a)(x+a) and expands to x2+2ax+a2; it is not x2+a2
By the end you can
G4Expand and simplify the product of two linear expressions.
G4Expand a squared bracket and use the expansion to write down the area of a square of side (x+a).

Every term in the first bracket multiplies every term in the second. So (x+3)(x+5) has four products, one in each cell of the grid.

Collect the two middle terms

The two x terms are like terms, so they are collected. (x+3)(x+5) =x2+8x+15, where 8 is 3+5 and 15 is 3×5
